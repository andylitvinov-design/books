## Independence of method stages and information coverage
The six informational dimensions are NOT six ordered stages and are NOT a requirement to complete every optional item. The Director's existing evidence-ordered method steps alone authorize stage progression. Model messages, time spent, transcript length, a percentage or browser-supplied claims cannot advance a stage.

A method-specific field has one of three statuses:
- missing: neither a substantive client-authored source quote nor relevant Director flag;
- partial: quote OR flag exists but the required evidence is incomplete;
- supported: suitable client-quote pattern AND all preexisting Director evidence signals are present.

Supported is NOT proven truthful, relevant to the present goal, client-verified, or safe to act upon. Quote relevance is explicitly unverified pending contextual confirmation. A follow-up question should be concise, optional and aligned to the current Director-authorized step. Corrections and topic changes invalidate previous-topic evidence. No new AI model calls.
