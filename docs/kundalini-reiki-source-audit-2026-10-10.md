## Scope and separation

This **independent three-level Kundalini Reiki course** is not a renamed Yggdrasil module or the nine-level Tantra Reiki program. It is placed as its own Academy card and Reiki course, and the historical PsiMaster source record is kept intact.

Six original first-party pages from three websites were verified and linked from the course. The new page is a **curated original-language adaptation in EN/RU/ES**, not a verbatim copy of all source text.

## Curriculum reconciliation

- **Level 1 — self-practice:** the basic meditation, attention to breath and sensation, gassho, simple self-hand placement, written observation, contact practice with consent and optional imagined distance focus
- **Level 2 — Balance:** Kundalini Flame symbolism, chakra attention and the ~30-second touching-fingertips gesture, explicitly described as a contemplative ritual instead of validated therapy
- **Level 3 — Master:** teaching/initiation model, plus historical Diamond, Crystal, DNA, Birth Trauma, Location and Past Life names; eight-day source exercise names preserved **as historic symbolic study**, never as treatments or biological claims
- **Practice method:** self-study → optional teacher initiation → partner exercise with consent → reflection and teacher feedback
- **Three-language UX:** a short introduction, collapsible per-level detailed syllabus, concise day-to-day cards, FAQ, sources, and named WhatsApp enquiries

## Editorial / safety decisions

Not republished as factual claims: "300 times stronger", guaranteed energy effects, psychic ability, medical healing, DNA repair, correction of birth trauma, proof of past lives, guaranteed success in money or relationships, or advice to replace medical care. Historical "free", "$1", "$10", certificate, and other promotions do not prove current terms. No automatic booking or existing class date is claimed.

Original third-party PDF \`http://www.bahaistudies.net/asma/kundalini_reiki_manual.pdf\` is linked from the original SuperSkills page, but **not** copied or uploaded to the Holistic House repository.

Historical remote-session and person-directed language is rewritten as optional visualization, never an assertion of external intervention. Consent is compulsory for real partner contact. The page does not invent prices or medical protocols.
