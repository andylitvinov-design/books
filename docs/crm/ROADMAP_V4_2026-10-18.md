# Holistic House CRM — Quality Roadmap v4
Version: 18 October 2026 · Target: https://holistichouse.vercel.app · Source: andylitvinov-design/books / codex/public-book-library

## Objective and boundaries
A calm, effective, mobile-first CRM for the Holistic House business enquiry journey. The owner should be able to locate a lead, answer a legitimate enquiry, remember the next action, and understand the difference between owner-reported and externally verified bookings, revenues and ad conversions. Always support EN/RU/ES.

Keep business CRM separate from client tests, medical information, consultations, private reports, study materials and payment entitlements. Never import sensitive client records or infer consent from account creation or subscriptions. No unsolicited mass sending.

## Baseline verified on 18 October
Already live in the source: owner-only encrypted contacts, manually supplied sources, email/phone-first leads, de-duplication, lead/client/archive lifecycle, immutable activity, overdue and due-today followups, three CRM tabs, consent-aware email and private messenger modules with default-off provider gates, business consulting enquiries, marketing analytics restricted to owner-verified commercial events. Correcting addresses cannot move an existing verified email permission. Owner CRM originally displayed 50 contact cards at a time, and its text filter searched only those loaded cards.

Unfinished independent provider gate: site has no verified Resend domain/key and no configured official messaging provider keys. Source-only integrations cannot establish actual delivery, invoices or ads.

## Delivery program

### Phase 0 — preserve live safety and quality
Maintain verified Supabase session, owner allowlist and anti-stale account header, with all reads/writes under restricted PostgreSQL hh_crm_backend and FORCE RLS. Protect prior email consent and permanent suppression, and phone numbers tied to messenger threads. Keep synthetic rollback QA, translation and accessibility parity. Do not automatically import clinical or billing data. No live messages or ads.

### Phase 1 — operational owner tools (this PR)
- On-demand private server search across encrypted business contacts by name, email or phone. Query only inside the verified owner transaction. Search up to 3,000 most recent owner-owned contacts per call, return up to 80 matches, and clearly disclose partial results. Filter by lifecycle before scanning; no searchable plaintext index or logging of queries. Future large-dataset blind index requires separate review.
- User-confirmed CSV download of owner business contacts, max 5,000 records. Only name, contact channels, selected source, language, lifecycle, verification/suppression flags, creation date and opaque contact ID. Never include psychological data, private cabinet links, test answers or personal recommendations.
- Neutralize spreadsheet formula injection and CSV linebreaks. Never create a permanent public file or store a downloaded copy on Drive/GitHub.
- Store an immutable audit entry with owner, UTC time, contact count, export format/scope; no personal data in the audit table. Show last 5 export events. If size exceeds cap, fail closed rather than silently truncate.
- Preserve existing section tabs, role checks, follow-up controls and EN/RU/ES. Public access must return 401.

### Phase 2 — fully consistent customer journey (future separate release)
- Unite inbound consultation, Reiki and business enquiries into a clear triage queue with response-age and due-date indicators. Link only identical verified business identities with consent; do not merge client cabinet records or assessment histories by guessing email.
- One-touch response/task actions for owner: reply, requested follow-up, mark booking discussed vs actually booked. Do not count lifecycle flags or course starts as verified paid conversions.
- Privacy-preserving blind-index directory search and stronger pagination when the volume passes 3,000. Include consent-safe reindexing and key rotation.
- Real source/offer attribution from immutable website events, quality checks for duplicate leads and sample-based reporting.

### Phase 3 — governance and verified revenue (review gates)
- Owner-controlled contact retention and export/delete workflow with documented CASL/financial record exceptions and bounded archive policy. No silent purge or irreversible CRM merges.
- Verified booking/order references with approved payment processor. Show paid vs pending vs refunded only from authoritative events; advertising ROI only with observed spend and conversion sources.
- Practitioner role expansion only after tested multitenant authorization, privacy disclosures and signed-in E2E.
- Production checks for latency, failure rates, PII-safe logs, backup/restore drills and accessibility across desktop and mobile.

### External launch gates (remain OFF)
- Resend: owner-controlled domain, DNS/SPF/DKIM verification, server-only API key/webhook secret, sender postal/contact details, consent/verification and synthetic delivery smoke; see issue #980.
- Telegram/WhatsApp/Instagram/Messenger: official business accounts, signed webhooks, customer-initiated messaging windows and observed valid provider receipts. No stock keys, automatic marketing or messages to real clients.
- Payments and ads: approval of accounts, amounts and connection, correct conversions and distinct consent; do not invent delivery or revenue data.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Owner search can retrieve a synthetic match outside the first 50 loaded contacts; reports incomplete search when above the 3,000 scan cap.
2. 100% of exports are initiated by explicit owner action and audited (time and count); no PII in audit, no formula cells executed on CSV opening, no file on a public URL.
3. No SELECT/INSERT for anon/authenticated on new audit table, FORCE RLS, no UPDATE/DELETE on audit.
4. Existing owner CRM authentication and three tabs remain intact, including phone-first leads, activity history and messenger inbox.
5. Production deployment exact SHA READY; anonymous API GET remains HTTP 401. Owner signed-in CRUD/export E2E remains a separate verification until a connected owner browser session exists.

## Completion markers
The roadmap is a living plan, not a claim that future phases or external providers are already activated. Record migration version, merged PR, live deployment and remaining QA gaps in the PR release notes.
