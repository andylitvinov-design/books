# MyTube content taxonomy v2 — 4 × 4 × 4

Status: editorial master catalogue; not published to MyTube and not wired into the live editor.

## Fixed hierarchy

| Primary category | Subcategories |
|---|---|
| Goals & Money | Goals & Discipline; Purpose & Direction; Money & Mindset; Business & Career |
| Love & Relationships | Finding Love; Couple Relationships; Loneliness & Breakups; Intimacy & Family |
| Energy & Confidence | Body & Wellbeing; Energy & Fatigue; Confidence & Self-Esteem; Sleep & Rest |
| Stress & Recovery | Stress & Burnout; Anxiety & Fears; Trauma & Childhood; Depression & Apathy |

Each subcategory contains four distinct topics and a localized RU/EN/ES chooseWhen hint explaining when that branch is the right choice. Every topic has four editorial scenario concepts. Total: 4 categories, 16 subcategories, 64 topics, 256 scenario concepts.

## Non-overlap rules

1. Every topic has exactly one primary location in the tree. Other related intents are search tags, not duplicate topics.
2. Money-related worry belongs to Money & Mindset when money is the trigger; general anxiety belongs to Anxiety & Panic.
3. Fatigue and low energy belong to Energy & Fatigue. Work-related burnout belongs to Stress & Burnout.
4. Self-confidence and self-esteem belong to Confidence & Self-Esteem. General anxiety, panic, social anxiety and specific phobias all belong to the single Anxiety & Fears branch.
5. Sleep difficulties belong to Sleep & Rest even when stress contributes to them.
6. Relationship breakups belong to Love & Relationships. Childhood rejection and abandonment wounds belong to Trauma & Childhood; low mood, apathy and emotional emptiness belong to Depression & Apathy.
7. Methods (meditation, hypnosis, Reiki, psychotherapy, guided imagery, constellations, breathwork and sound) are separate filters, never need-based categories.
8. Depression-related videos are supportive content, not diagnosis or treatment claims.

## Language and search metadata

Every topic includes RU, EN and ES names and natural search-intent phrases. Every subcategory includes an RU/EN/ES choice hint so visitors can distinguish it from neighboring branches. These are editorial keyword candidates, not verified search-volume metrics. Validate regional volume separately with Google Keyword Planner and Yandex Wordstat before publishing SEO landing pages.

## Scenario distinction

Each topic has four scenario concepts with four distinct creative approaches: journey, support, reframe and integration. They are editorial briefs, not export-ready scripts or finished video files. The current legacy five-category video taxonomy and its stable scenario IDs remain unchanged; this v2 file is a separate MyTube content catalogue pending a separately reviewed UI integration.

## Subcategory choice hints

Every subcategory carries a short localized RU/EN/ES `chooseWhen` hint. The hint tells the visitor which primary need belongs there and helps distinguish neighboring branches. For example: Energy & Fatigue is for low energy and physical/mental tiredness; Stress & Burnout is for prolonged overload and work-related burnout. Sleep & Rest is selected when sleep itself is the main problem, even if stress contributes to it.
