# MyTube canonical category structure

Status: implementation PR; editorial master catalogue is separate from the currently live video pipeline.

## Agreed public hierarchy

| Main category | Four subcategories |
|---|---|
| Деньги и развитие / Goals & Development | Предназначение и развитие; Достижение целей; Деньги и изобилие; Бизнес и карьера |
| Любовь и отношения / Love & Relationships | Одиночество и расставание; Поиск любви; Отношения в паре; Близость и семья |
| Энергия и уверенность / Energy & Confidence | Стресс и усталость; Тело и здоровье; Жизненная энергия; Уверенность и спокойствие |
| Стресс и тревога / Stress & Anxiety | Стресс и выгорание; Тревога и страхи; Психологические травмы; Депрессия и апатия |

Each subcategory has four focused themes. Each theme has four scenario-card concepts. Total: 4 categories, 16 subcategories, 64 themes and 256 scenario cards.

## Clear routing boundaries

- **Stress & Fatigue** is for everyday tiredness, end-of-day overload and recovery after exertion. **Stress & Burnout** is for prolonged psychological pressure and work-related burnout.
- **Energy & Fatigue** is for lack of physical or mental energy; **Vital Energy** is for vitality, morning activation and mental clarity.
- **Confidence & Calm** is for self-confidence, self-esteem, acceptance and composure in important situations.
- **Anxiety & Fears** covers ongoing worry, panic, social anxiety and specific phobias.
- **Psychological Trauma** is for painful past experiences and childhood patterns.
- **Depression & Apathy** contains supportive content around low mood, apathy, loss of joy and emotional emptiness.

Every topic has exactly one primary location. Related search phrases are metadata, not duplicate branches. Methods (meditation, hypnosis, Reiki, psychotherapy, guided imagery, constellations, breathwork and sound) remain independent filters.

## Scenario cards

The four card approaches are:
1. A New Path — move from the selected topic toward one achievable step.
2. A Place to Stand — supportive imagery and resources.
3. A New Perspective — a topic-specific symbolic reframe.
4. A First Step — an optional everyday integration.

These are story concepts, not finished scripts, licensed assets or export-ready videos. Export remains disabled until real media and rights are verified.

## Compatibility

The site and MyTube use the new visible four-category tree. Existing historical tags, profile scores, legacy 5×6×4 renderer IDs and already stored user drafts remain compatible through a crosswalk. They are not silently renamed or deleted.
