## Scope

This audit was performed after the original Academy migration and the subsequent sparse-content recoveries.

Checked:

- **42 / 42** public PsiTrends Academy/training source records from `data/academy/sources.generated.json`.
- **26 / 26** backup-only records classified as `Academy` in `data/academy/preservation.generated.json`.

## Safety / publication rules applied

The migration preserves historical educational structure without silently promoting old claims as current facts.

Filtered or reframed where needed:

- stale prices and registration offers
- qualification/certificate promises
- medical/cure claims
- guaranteed business or personal outcomes
- strong supernatural-effect claims
- old event dates/locations presented as if current
- broken `Loading...` / deprecated-renderer output

No new HeyGen generation or media upload was performed.
