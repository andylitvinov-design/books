# Holistic House — My Course · Key needs and flagship 14-day tracks

**Product and editorial passport · 13 October 2026.** EN/RU, accessible mobile-first, 5–12 minutes/day. Source of truth: `lib/inner-growth/course-programs.js` (42 distinct bilingual daily accents); underlying therapeutic-methodology content is already versioned and must not be replaced for active clients.

## Customer demand and pain map

**Priority #1: Stress / everyday overload** — “I cannot switch off” / «Не получается расслабиться». This is the broadest likely initial acquisition need. Desired outcome: approachable pause, emotional steadiness, recovery.

**Priority #2: Confidence / self-support** — “I keep doubting myself” / «Постоянно сомневаюсь в себе». High personal salience, plausible connection to individualized sessions. Desired outcome: self-support, expressing needs, respectful boundaries.

**Priority #3: Focus / energy / action** — “I feel scattered and stuck” / «Трудно собраться и начать действовать». Productivity and work focus attract a distinct audience. Desired outcome: small sustainable actions, attention routines, realistic energy planning.

**Follow-on demand:** sleep/recovery and relationships/connection, after first three tracks are tested. Not separate clinical products yet.

**Underlying three-layer motivation:** immediate relief from a difficult state → practical daily skills and improved agency → felt inner support, personal choice and acceptance.

These priorities are strategic hypotheses, **not measured search volume, paid conversion, or proven treatment effects** on Holistic House. Validate using real, consent-compliant product analytics.

**Promise:** “Feel calmer. Grow stronger. See your progress.” / «Больше спокойствия. Больше опоры. Замечайте изменения». Position the course as gentle, self-directed psychological-skills training, not an “assessment questionnaire” and not a substitute for professional care.

## What users see and do

1. First viewport: one compact hero, one recognisable pain-led triad: **Stress Relief**, **Inner Confidence**, **Focus & Energy**, plus **Personal course**. No long text before the first CTA.
2. Click a programme: explicit consent for private storage; existing account-owned `start` → `intake` path saves only previously supported topical tags and advances to real tests. Personal choice retains the original manual intake.
3. Programme starter battery: prefer three catalog-approved instruments and show a total of five reviewed recommendations. All tests must already be rights-cleared and selectable from the existing full catalogue. User confirms the battery; a preset **does not** imply completed tests or consent.
4. After tests: suggest **three** compatible measured and directionally eligible scale areas, with programme-specific relevance order. The learner retains control to change them.
5. Daily: existing 14-day owned lessons, local day gate, 1 meaningful unit per slide, brief micro-action and optional now-before/now-after observation. Label today's programme and describe a **single** target micro-action, not a wall of text.
6. Paths: 14 daily nodes show the programme's reviewed themes and short prompts; original core lessons and immutable version IDs remain. Day 7/14 feedback and day-14 optional same-test repeat continue under the original cooldown/compatibility rules.

## 14-day course passports

The same four-stage learning architecture applies across all tracks: **days 1–3 understand patterns; days 4–7 practise support; days 8–11 use skills in real life; days 12–14 integrate and continue**. Each track accent below is additional reviewed editorial guidance, not a replacement for version-pinned safe exercises.

### Stress Relief / Антистресс

**Need:** pressure, worry and difficulty switching off. **Learning spine:** notice triggers, make room for a pause, practise sustainable self-support. **Skills:** calm regulation, emotional flexibility and recovery.

| Day | English focus | Русская тема | Daily micro-action |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notice your stress pattern | Замечаем свой стресс | Name one everyday situation where tension shows up. |
| 2 | Recognize early signals | Замечаем первые сигналы | Notice one early sign without trying to change it. |
| 3 | Unhook from pressure | Не сливаться с давлением | Give a recurring stressful thought a simple name. |
| 4 | Find your grounding cue | Находим точку опоры | Look around and find three neutral objects. |
| 5 | Make room for a pause | Разрешаем себе паузу | Try one short pause before an ordinary response. |
| 6 | Speak to yourself gently | Мягкая внутренняя поддержка | Find a phrase that feels supportive and believable. |
| 7 | Review what fits | Смотрим, что помогает | Keep one helpful practice; change anything uncomfortable. |
| 8 | Choose a flexible response | Выбираем новую реакцию | Notice one choice available when you feel under pressure. |
| 9 | Make an if–then plan | Составляем план «если — то» | If tension rises, then try your familiar grounding cue. |
| 10 | Try it in real life | Пробуем в обычном дне | Use the small pause in one low-pressure situation. |
| 11 | Protect a recovery window | Выделяем время на восстановление | Make room for one realistic break today. |
| 12 | Accept available support | Принимаем доступную помощь | Identify one person or place where support is available. |
| 13 | Build your calm toolkit | Собираем набор спокойствия | Choose two gentle actions you can use again. |
| 14 | Review and continue | Подводим итоги и продолжаем | Notice what changed or stayed the same; plan your next step. |

### Inner Confidence / Внутренняя уверенность

**Need:** fear of evaluation, self-criticism and difficulty asserting preferences. **Learning spine:** notice the inner critic, find a believable supportive voice, practise small respectful choices and boundaries. **Skills:** inner support, communication and emotional flexibility.

| Day | English focus | Русская тема | Daily micro-action |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose a real-life goal | Выбираем жизненную цель | Identify one situation where you want more choice. |
| 2 | Spot your inner critic | Замечаем внутреннего критика | Notice one self-critical phrase without treating it as a fact. |
| 3 | Separate fact from judgment | Отделяем факт от оценки | Write one observation without adding a harsh label. |
| 4 | Find inner support | Находим внутреннюю опору | Identify one quality or person that helps you feel supported. |
| 5 | Try a kinder alternative | Ищем мягкую альтернативу | Replace one rigid “must” with a believable “could”. |
| 6 | Express a small preference | Говорим о своём выборе | Try saying one ordinary preference respectfully. |
| 7 | Review without grading | Подводим итоги без оценок | Keep what helped; skip what felt too hard. |
| 8 | Act from what matters | Действуем из ценностей | Choose one tiny action that reflects a personal value. |
| 9 | Practise a gentle boundary | Практикуем мягкую границу | Rehearse “I need a moment” in a safe context. |
| 10 | Notice courage in action | Замечаем смелость в действии | Observe one small choice despite ordinary doubt. |
| 11 | Ask for practical support | Просим о поддержке | Make one small, optional request to a safe person. |
| 12 | Build a self-support toolkit | Собираем инструменты опоры | Write down two phrases or actions that feel yours. |
| 13 | Choose without perfection | Выбираем без идеальности | Use a realistic “good enough” decision rule. |
| 14 | Review your own voice | Замечаем свой голос | Name one choice that felt more like your own. |

### Focus & Energy / Фокус и энергия

**Need:** scattered attention, low energy, trouble initiating tasks. **Learning spine:** see common distractions, protect energy, choose one realistic next action, create sustainable cues. **Skills:** clarity, agency and self-pacing.

| Day | English focus | Русская тема | Daily micro-action |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notice your attention | Наблюдаем внимание | Choose one everyday task where focus matters. |
| 2 | Map your distractions | Замечаем отвлечения | Notice one common distraction without blaming yourself. |
| 3 | Match your energy | Учитываем свои силы | Identify a time of day when small actions feel easier. |
| 4 | Choose one priority | Выбираем один приоритет | Write a single realistic priority for today. |
| 5 | Start for two minutes | Начинаем с двух минут | Try a very short first step instead of a perfect plan. |
| 6 | Design a simple cue | Создаём подсказку | Place one gentle reminder near your task. |
| 7 | Review your energy | Смотрим на свою энергию | Keep what worked and permit a slower pace. |
| 8 | Link focus to a value | Связываем фокус с ценностью | Choose a tiny task connected to what matters to you. |
| 9 | Make an if–then plan | Планируем «если — то» | If you notice distraction, then return to one next step. |
| 10 | Try one focus block | Пробуем один блок фокуса | Complete an optional short single-task interval. |
| 11 | Recover between efforts | Восстанавливаемся между делами | Try a brief screen-free pause after effort. |
| 12 | Choose a sustainable pace | Выбираем устойчивый темп | Alternate doable actions and permitted recovery. |
| 13 | Create a Plan B | Создаём запасной план | Decide your smallest next step for low-energy days. |
| 14 | Review and adjust | Подводим итоги и корректируем | Notice what fits your life, without promising perfect focus. |

## One-day lesson contract and outcome integrity

One local calendar day = at most one **new** saved session. Voluntary last-24-hour personal 0–10 check-in and momentary before/after exercise ratings are separate; avoid forced rating and avoid claiming clinical validity or causality. A short reflection, readable guided script, micro-action, safe low-intensity alternative, optional written note, a private progress chart and server-confirmed completion are standard for all tracks. Existing baseline questionnaires may be repeated after Day 14 when compatible and legally/clinically eligible; never fabricate changes or re-label incompatible results.

**Content safety:** no diagnosis, prescribing, dosages, cure guarantees or claims of replacing urgent/professional help. Exercises are optional, not forced exposure or trauma excavation. Eyes can stay open and breathing natural. Decrease intensity or stop when uncomfortable.
