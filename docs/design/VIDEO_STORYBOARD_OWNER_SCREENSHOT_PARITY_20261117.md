## One canonical contract
- A selected or approved 30-second film has **six distinct 5-second events**, in the same order and wording in Ideas and Montage. Changing a creative direction changes both.
- Montage displays the **selected film title**, the explicit action and timecode of each shot; optional camera framing and emotional function are secondary, not replacement generic labels.
- The limited five-shot legacy format is **20 seconds only** and must not supersede a six-shot screenplay.
- A source-image thumbnail is shown only when it refers to a verified media item. Film footage quality, technical suitability, copyrights and *depicted physical action* are separate questions. A generic landscape must not be presented as evidence of suitcase/photograph/phone/meeting.
- A manually rewritten Main Idea, Concept Story or any shot invalidates prior editorial approval until six shot actions have been reviewed; editing a shot clears stale generic scene keywords.
- Preapproval remains available in free guest mode for valid films. Voice, manually selected clips, captions and music are retained; nothing silently charges or starts a paid provider.
