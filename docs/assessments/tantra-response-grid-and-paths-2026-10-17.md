# Tantra self-reflection: scales, descriptive levels and test paths

Date: 2026-10-17. Holistic House online test catalog. **Non-clinical educational self-reflection for adults.**

## What the numbers actually measure

- Each original Tantra questionnaire is scored **separately by construct**, on the versioned 0–4 question scale, using the arithmetic mean of its specific registered items.
- The results do **not** measure tantric ability, a chakra energy level, kundalini activation, sexual functioning, consent, physical safety, spiritual rank, disease severity, or clinical improvement.
- Every original HH mean scale with five source answer anchors shows exactly five **descriptive response-position intervals**. They are nearest-original-answer groups, **not normed psychometric cutoffs**:

| Average response | EN interpretation | RU interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to below 0.5 | Rarely reported | Почти не отмечается |
| 0.5 to below 1.5 | Occasionally reported | Отмечается редко |
| 1.5 to below 2.5 | Depends on context | Зависит от ситуации |
| 2.5 to below 3.5 | Often reported | Отмечается часто |
| 3.5 to 4 inclusive | Consistently reported | Отмечается устойчиво |

These labels are *descriptive* even when a spiritual framework provides a traditional chakra name or L1–L5 workshop theme. They are neither pass/fail nor healthy/unhealthy.

For each instrument and subscale the report displays:
1. Exact score, original possible minimum/maximum, original response option closest to the mean;
2. The highlighted corresponding descriptive interval;
3. A two-column desktop / one-column mobile grid showing **all five intervals**, with a construct-specific explanation for each;
4. The existing authored optional step for that subscale; compatible historical results can be compared only with the same version and identity.

Published, validated clinical screeners stay on their own source-backed categories. An unverified or altered saved result gets **no** invented cutoff, explanatory band or personalized interpretation.

## Curated opt-in test pathways

These are not composite scores, subscriptions, courses, diagnoses or automatic enrolments. Clicking a pathway only changes the **unsaved** test selection; starting a runner requires a separate user action. Only currently verified, cleared and selectable test keys appear.

- **Begin with presence:** existing 6-question Tantric Presence.
- **Body & sensitivity:** the original sensory/energy reflection plus Sensual Joy.
- **Heart & connection:** the tantric connection and emotional intimacy reflections.
- **Choice & boundaries:** boundaries/consent and trust dialogue self-reflections; not a determination of anyone's consent.
- **Freedom & self-acceptance:** shame-aware self-reflection with no expected desire level.
- **Seven symbolic chakras:** seven self-report life themes; no energy measurement.
- **Tantra L1–L5:** safety, heart, liberation, softening tension and embodiment are workshop topics, not awarded grades.
- **Integration & aftercare:** existing Weekly Pulse plus Body Signals; repeated questionnaires observe change, not causation or treatment effect.
- **Complete 64-item portrait:** eight independent self-report dimensions, no fabricated overall tantric percentage.

## High-value additions to consider after this release

**Priority A: After-practice check-in design.** Separate questions on feelings of support, overstimulation and voluntary stopping, with the ability to skip. Use an original, carefully reviewed questionnaire and a new immutable definition version. Do not auto-diagnose trauma or imply that an exercise caused a change.

**Priority B: Shame and social-evaluation reflection.** A separate opt-in questionnaire for embarrassment and fear of judgement in personal contact. Avoid asking for abuse disclosures without an appropriate clinical protocol; explicit trauma screening belongs outside hobby questionnaires.

**Priority C: External research reference and original PDFs.** Continue metadata-first for Tantric Sex Scale, Sexual Mindfulness Measure, MAIA-2, Scale of Body Connection, ECR-R and NSSS. Obtain exact permissions for online/commercial use, translations, distribution, and instrument-level scoring before enabling forms. Official MAIA-2 source and permission terms: https://osher.ucsf.edu/research/maia . Validated MAIA-2 research publication: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6279042/ .

## Release verification checklist

- EN/RU originals; no invented ES translation of copyrighted items.
- Every dimension for the six Tantra questionnaires plus legacy Tantric Presence has all five narrative levels.
- Boundaries 0, 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5 and 4 match the selected position. Original answer anchors remain immutable.
- No medical cutoff and no arbitrary "good/bad" or percent-health evaluation.
- Existing guest score-only results remain non-diagnostic, saved account reports remain private.
- Every pathway preserves independent source versions, rights status and all user-owned saved data.
- The published application commit and live UX require verification beyond a merged PR.
