# English adult trauma-screening specifications
Verified 2026-10-14. Research package only: no repository, registry, database or client data changed.

## Conclusion
All three exact English instruments have explicit public-domain declarations from their official owners/developers. They can be considered for commercial electronic reproduction on that basis; this is an inference from public-domain status, not a separate commercial-platform approval or clinical certification. Retain attribution. VA says its measures are intended for qualified health professionals and researchers. Suitable for a professionally supported adult screening/symptom-monitoring workflow after implementation QA; not an automatic consumer diagnosis service. None is marked implementation-ready here because no site integration or clinical workflow was inspected.

The JSON files contain 20 PCL-5 symptom items, 5 PC-PTSD-5 symptom items plus the exposure gate, and 18 adult ITQ items (12 symptoms and 6 functional-impairment items). Wording was extracted from official source files; whitespace only normalized. Original PDFs/DOCX and SHA-256 hashes are included. PCL Criterion A original PDF/text are also included, but that front matter has not been normalized to JSON: use it as the source if choosing that form.

## Critical scoring and administration distinctions
### PCL-5
Use past month, anchored to the worst event. Twenty integer responses 0–4; total 0–80. Cluster B=1–5 (0–20), C=6–7 (0–8), D=8–14 (0–28), E=15–20 (0–24). No reversals, transformations or rounding. A symptom is endorsed at >=2; the symptom-pattern rule requires >=1 B, >=1 C, >=2 D, >=2 E. This does not establish all clinical diagnostic requirements. VA discusses 31–33 as a possible probable-PTSD cutoff range, dependent on sample and purpose; no HH cutoff selected. Do not invent mild/moderate/severe bands: the VA guide explicitly states there are no empirically derived severity ranges.

The standard form is appropriate only when trauma exposure is assessed separately. Otherwise use the exact Criterion A front matter, including exposure type and the distinction between accidental/violent and natural death of a close person. Do not substitute a vague stress question. Do not impose PC-PTSD's exposure=no→0 rule on PCL-5. The instrument lacks a complete diagnostic interview, and standard 20-item scoring does not contain an impairment criterion. Adult evidence on the VA page includes university students, veterans and service members; applicability to the site's actual population still requires clinical review. Do not interchange PCL-IV and PCL-5 scores or past-week and past-month forms.

### PC-PTSD-5
Lifetime exposure gate first. NO means stop and official screen total 0; the five symptoms are structurally unasked, not five answered NOs. YES means ask all five binary questions about the past month and count YES, range 0–5. A missing gate is not NO. No subscales or reverse items. The 2022 source describes cutoff 4 in VA primary care with sex-dependent sensitivity limitations; that evidence does not select a universal public-site threshold. Positive screening requires further assessment. Primary validation: [Prins et al. 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5023594/); updated VA primary-care accuracy study: [Bovin et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7862990/).

### ITQ adult
P1–P6 refer to past-month symptom bother. C1–C6 refer to typical emotional, self-concept and relationship patterns, not simply past-month bother. P7–P9 and C7–C9 ask past-month impairment associated with their respective preceding sections. Preserve these different instructions. Six two-item symptom clusters each range 0–8. PTSD symptom total P1–P6=0–24; DSO symptom total C1–C6=0–24. Do not add impairment items to symptom totals. A 12-symptom sum 0–48 is used in developer research, but is not a diagnostic cutoff.

Endorsement threshold is >=2 for symptoms AND impairment. Important extraction hazard: the DOCX uses an UNDERLINED '>' character to represent greater-than-or-equal. Plain text extraction falsely displays '> 2'. Ten underlined signs were verified in the XML. The developer-authored [Cloitre et al. 2021 study, Measures §1.3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8221157/) expressly confirms >=2. Never implement >2.

PTSD requirements = any P1/P2 AND any P3/P4 AND any P5/P6 AND any P7/P8/P9 endorsed. DSO requirements = any C1/C2 AND any C3/C4 AND any C5/C6 AND any C7/C8/C9 endorsed. CPTSD pattern requires both PTSD and DSO requirements; PTSD-only pattern requires PTSD and NOT DSO. Mutually exclusive. DSO alone is neither pattern. These are self-report algorithm outputs for professional review, not established diagnoses. Qualifying trauma exposure and clinical relevance must also be considered; do not invent a DSM-5 gate for this ICD-11 measure. The exact form anchors to the most troubling experience and its timing.

Adult evidence: [original developer validation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30178492/), UK trauma-exposed community and clinical samples. The developer 2021 study included trauma-exposed US veterans aged 18+ using a web intervention; this supports relevant adult use but does not validate HH's specific workflow or every population. No child form is included.

## Missingness and remaining gates
No individual-level imputation/prorating permission was located in these official forms/guides. Proposed conservative software behavior: unanswered=null; only calculate from all applicable complete responses; otherwise unavailable. This is an explicitly labelled engineering handling choice, not an invented official missing-data rule. Structural PC-PTSD skipping is a separate official exception. Reject out-of-range, fractional and malformed responses, and do not coerce missing/empty strings to zero.

Before activation: verify site branching and distinct sections; confirm clinical oversight and follow-up presentation; compare rendered items against originals; test synthetic boundaries; confirm age/language population fit; verify no existing registry conflict, then verify any future authorized write by readback. No Russian item version or translation validity was audited. A Russian UI label is not a validated Russian assessment. Sensitive free-text trauma narratives should be optional/minimized, with storage choices addressed separately.
