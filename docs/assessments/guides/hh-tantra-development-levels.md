# Tantra Practice: Five Development Themes / Тантра: пять направлений практики

**Instrument ID:** `hh-tantra-development-levels`

**Type:** Original Holistic House adult educational self-reflection · EN/RU · v1.
**Questions:** 20. **Scoring:** each dimension has an independent mean of 0–4.

## What this test explores
Reflect on the Holistic House L1–L5 workshop themes: safety, heart, freedom, softening defences and embodied expression. Not a certification or rank.

### Что исследует тест
Исследуйте темы семинаров Holistic House L1–L5: безопасность, сердце, освобождение, смягчение защит и телесное выражение. Не аттестация и не рейтинг.

**Ethical use:** Not clinically validated, not a diagnosis, sexual performance judgement, consent assessment, chakras-energy measurement or ranking. Descriptive colours express answer position, not normative good/bad categories. Participation and exercises are optional.
