## Measured scales & interpretation

### Social / emotional awareness / Социальная и эмоциональная чуткость
- Score: arithmetic mean of 4 official 1–5 responses, after reversing: viar.ipip_via_r_social_intelligence_4.03, viar.ipip_via_r_social_intelligence_4.04. Expected range 1–5.
- Interpretation: descriptive personal tendency only; a lower or higher value is not inherently good/bad and is not a clinical cutoff.
- EN recommendation: Notice when asking a direct question clarifies someone's feelings better than trying to guess them.
- RU рекомендация: Отметьте, когда прямой уважительный вопрос лучше помогает понять чувства, чем догадки.
