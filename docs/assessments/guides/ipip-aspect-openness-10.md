## Measured scales & interpretation

### Openness to experience / Открытость переживаниям
- Score: arithmetic mean of 10 official 1–5 responses, after reversing: bfas.ipip_aspect_openness_10.07, bfas.ipip_aspect_openness_10.08, bfas.ipip_aspect_openness_10.09, bfas.ipip_aspect_openness_10.10. Expected range 1–5.
- Interpretation: descriptive personal tendency only; a lower or higher value is not inherently good/bad and is not a clinical cutoff.
- EN recommendation: Notice which creative or reflective activity genuinely restores your interest, without forcing it.
- RU рекомендация: Отметьте занятия, где творчество или размышление естественно возвращают интерес, без принуждения.
