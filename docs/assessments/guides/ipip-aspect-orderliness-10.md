## Measured scales & interpretation

### Orderliness / Любовь к порядку
- Score: arithmetic mean of 10 official 1–5 responses, after reversing: bfas.ipip_aspect_orderliness_10.07, bfas.ipip_aspect_orderliness_10.08, bfas.ipip_aspect_orderliness_10.09, bfas.ipip_aspect_orderliness_10.10. Expected range 1–5.
