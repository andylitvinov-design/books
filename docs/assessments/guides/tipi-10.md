## Measured scales & interpretation

### Extraversion / Экстраверсия
- Score: arithmetic mean of 2 official 1–7 responses, after reversing: tipi.tipi_10.06. Expected range 1–7.
- Interpretation: descriptive personal tendency only; a lower or higher value is not inherently good/bad and is not a clinical cutoff.
- EN recommendation: Notice where interaction energizes you and where solitude helps; both preferences can be healthy.
- RU рекомендация: Замечайте, когда общение прибавляет сил, а когда полезнее уединение; оба варианта нормальны.

### Agreeableness / Доброжелательность
- Score: arithmetic mean of 2 official 1–7 responses, after reversing: tipi.tipi_10.02. Expected range 1–7.
- Interpretation: descriptive personal tendency only; a lower or higher value is not inherently good/bad and is not a clinical cutoff.
- EN recommendation: Consider how cooperation and setting personal limits fit together in important relationships.
- RU рекомендация: Подумайте, как сотрудничество сочетается с личными границами в важных отношениях.

### Conscientiousness / Организованность
- Score: arithmetic mean of 2 official 1–7 responses, after reversing: tipi.tipi_10.08. Expected range 1–7.
- Interpretation: descriptive personal tendency only; a lower or higher value is not inherently good/bad and is not a clinical cutoff.
- EN recommendation: Look for routines that help with commitments without turning your day into a perfectionism test.
- RU рекомендация: Ищите привычки, которые поддерживают дела, не превращая день в экзамен на идеальность.

### Emotional stability / Эмоциональная устойчивость
- Score: arithmetic mean of 2 official 1–7 responses, after reversing: tipi.tipi_10.04. Expected range 1–7.
- Interpretation: descriptive personal tendency only; a lower or higher value is not inherently good/bad and is not a clinical cutoff.
- EN recommendation: Reflect on the context of emotional changes rather than treating sensitivity as a defect.
- RU рекомендация: Рассматривайте контекст эмоциональных перемен вместо того, чтобы считать чувствительность недостатком.

### Openness to experience / Открытость опыту
- Score: arithmetic mean of 2 official 1–7 responses, after reversing: tipi.tipi_10.10. Expected range 1–7.
- Interpretation: descriptive personal tendency only; a lower or higher value is not inherently good/bad and is not a clinical cutoff.
- EN recommendation: Notice which new activities inspire interest and where familiar approaches serve you well.
- RU рекомендация: Отмечайте, что новое пробуждает интерес и когда знакомые подходы полезнее.
