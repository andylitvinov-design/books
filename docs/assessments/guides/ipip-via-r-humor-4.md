### Humor / playfulness / Юмор и игривость
- Score: arithmetic mean of 4 official 1–5 responses, after reversing: viar.ipip_via_r_humor_4.03, viar.ipip_via_r_humor_4.04. Expected range 1–5.
- Interpretation: descriptive personal tendency only; a lower or higher value is not inherently good/bad and is not a clinical cutoff.
