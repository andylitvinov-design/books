# Sexual Freedom & Self-Acceptance / Сексуальная свобода и самопринятие

**Instrument ID:** `hh-sexual-freedom-inhibition`

**Type:** Original Holistic House adult educational self-reflection · 2026-10-17 · v1 · EN/RU.
**Length:** 12 self-report questions. **Scale:** mean response 0–4 per independent dimension.

## What this test explores

Non-judgmental reflection on body acceptance, shame and freely chosen preferences. Desire and sexual activity are never required.

### Что исследует тест
Бережная саморефлексия о принятии тела, стыде и свободном выборе. Желание и сексуальная активность не являются обязательными.

**Evidence and limitations / Ограничения:** An original non-validated self-reflection, not a clinical test, source-published psychometric norm or measure of sexual performance. A higher answer mean only describes greater agreement with its item wording. Chakra and kundalini language is symbolic, not the measurement of energy. Participation and any exercises are optional. Consent cannot be inferred from any questionnaire answer.
