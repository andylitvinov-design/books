# Sensuality & Everyday Joy / Чувственность и радость

**Instrument:** `hh-sensual-joy` · V1 · six original items, EN/RU · approximately 2 minutes · response 0–4 · timeframe: last 30 days.

**Status:** Original Holistic House educational self-reflection. Not a validated questionnaire, psychometric diagnosis, medical screening, sexuality diagnosis, attachment diagnosis, compatibility test or way to determine consent. No population norms, clinically validated thresholds or inference about another person.

## Purpose / Назначение

EN: Notice comfortable sensory pleasure and playfulness without equating sensuality with sex or requiring physical contact.

RU: Заметить приятные ощущения и живость без отождествления чувственности с сексом и без необходимости телесного контакта.

## Scored dimensions / Отдельные шкалы

### 1. Comfortable sensations / Комфортные ощущения

- Key: `resources.sensual_joy.senses`; items 1–3; mean, 0–4.
- Area: `embodiment`. Direction: higher reported access to a resource; not “better person.”
- EN reflection: Notice one pleasant everyday sensory detail for thirty seconds, such as music or a warm drink, if comfortable.
- RU практика: Если приятно, полминуты замечайте одну обычную радующую деталь — например музыку или тёплый напиток.

### 2. Unpressured enjoyment / Удовольствие без давления

- Key: `resources.sensual_joy.playfulness`; items 4–6; mean, 0–4.
- Area: `sexual_wellbeing`. Direction: higher reported access to a resource; not “better person.”
- EN reflection: Choose one enjoyable, non-demanding activity and notice what makes it feel voluntary and relaxed.
- RU практика: Выберите одно приятное действие без обязательств и отметьте, что делает его добровольным и расслабленным.

## Interpretation for every possible score

Each scored dimension is the mean of its three items. **0–4 is a descriptive range, not a clinical norm:** 0–<1⅓ is the lower third, 1⅓–2⅔ the middle third, >2⅔–4 the upper third. The result report interprets the exact score without diagnosing or assigning a sexual/relational label; it combines the dimension-specific reflection above with appropriate opt-in guidance for lower/middle/upper positions. Scale values can be compared only with this exact version at comparable timeframes. The client chooses whether to monitor a skill or repeat after 30 days.

EN: Sensuality can mean music, texture, warmth or beauty. Nothing needs to be intensified or shared with others.

RU: Чувственность может проявляться в музыке, фактуре, тепле или красоте. Ничего не нужно усиливать или разделять с другими.

## Safety, privacy and consent

Adults only. All questions are voluntary and users can skip an entire questionnaire. The Google-authenticated cabinet stores their own results under existing account access/RLS. Never share these as public files, predict a partner's intentions, assume sexual activity or suggest an ideal frequency of desire. Consent is situational and can be withdrawn: no score ever proves its presence. Sexual pain, health symptoms or sexual concerns require qualified medical assessment as appropriate; coercion or immediate danger warrants appropriate support rather than a test score. No promises of therapeutic effects from Tantra.
