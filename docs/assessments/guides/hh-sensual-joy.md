### 1. Comfortable sensations / Комфортные ощущения

- Key: `resources.sensual_joy.senses`; items 1–3; mean, 0–4.

## Interpretation for every possible score

Each scored dimension is the mean of its three items, rounded to two decimal places before report interpretation. **0–4 is a descriptive range, not a clinical norm:** the report compares the rounded value divided by 4 with 1/3 and 2/3. For the attainable scores in this version, 0.00–1.33 is the lower part, 1.67–2.33 the middle part, and 2.67–4.00 the upper part. In particular, a raw mean of 4/3 becomes 1.33 (lower), and 8/3 becomes 2.67 (upper). These labels are descriptive and are not clinical cutoffs. The result report interprets the exact score without diagnosing or assigning a sexual/relational label; it combines the dimension-specific reflection above with appropriate opt-in guidance for lower/middle/upper positions. Scale values can be compared only with this exact version at comparable timeframes. The client chooses whether to monitor a skill or repeat after 30 days.

## Safety, privacy and consent

Adults only. All questions are voluntary and users can skip an entire questionnaire. The Google-authenticated cabinet stores their own results under existing account access/RLS. Never share these as public files, predict a partner's intentions, assume sexual activity or suggest an ideal frequency of desire. Consent is situational and can be withdrawn: no score ever proves its presence. Sexual pain, health symptoms or sexual concerns require qualified medical assessment as appropriate; coercion or immediate danger warrants appropriate support rather than a test score. No promises of therapeutic effects from Tantra.
