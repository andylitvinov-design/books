- E1–E6 (six): friendliness, preference for groups, assertiveness, activity pace, excitement seeking, cheerfulness

**Exclusion:** O6 (Liberalism) contains explicit political-party voting-preference items. This is 29 individually published four-item facets, not the complete 120-item battery. Short facets vary in reliability (reported on original keys); scores are descriptive continuous means only.
