## Evidence and use cautions

- CPQ refers to the Lindsay *Change Process* measure; it is not the Communication Patterns Questionnaire CPQ-35 or the Clinical Perfectionism Questionnaire CPQ-12.
- CPQ 18-factor model was presented as preliminary in the original paper. Couples Relationship Scale 10 aspects do not imply 10 scored subscales.
- STIC is copyright/trademark protected by The Family Institute at Northwestern University. Its original free research, clinical and teaching use does not establish permission to reproduce the forms, scoring, or feedback website as commercial digital SaaS. Obtain owner authorization first.
- Source articles in English, Spanish or Chinese are **not** proof of a validated respondent questionnaire edition in those languages. No newly validated RU or ES respondent-language edition is claimed. Questionnaire items, algorithms, interpretation norms and cutoffs are intentionally excluded.
- The 2026-10-19 read-only Supabase snapshot counted 145 published instrument keys and 230 locale-version rows (EN 150 / RU 80 / ES 0); no candidate keyed from this addendum was registered.
- This file does **not** change the app, data definitions, clinical form registry or Supabase; it is not a deployment approval. Google Sheets 01–08 and 11 unchanged.
