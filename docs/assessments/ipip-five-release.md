# Second IPIP preparation batch: five meaningful gaps

Five original-English IPIP scales complement the three-scale cognitive-failures, optimism and attending-to-emotions group.

## Why these constructs

Three distinct control-belief scales address personal agency, institutional/interpersonal influence and chance in everyday and work decisions. They are not equivalent to the site's self-esteem, resilience, optimism or personality inventories. Positive and Negative Expressivity address how feelings are expressed in relationships, distinct from the existing ERQ reappraisal/suppression scales. No aggregate locus-of-control or emotional-intelligence score is manufactured.

## Exact forms and source evidence

- IPIP Locus of Control: Internality, 10 items: positive positions 1–6, reverse 7–10; sum 10–50.
- IPIP Locus of Control: Powerful Others, 10 items: positive positions 1–6, reverse 7–10; sum 10–50.
- IPIP Locus of Control: Chance, 10 items: positive positions 1–6, reverse 7–10; sum 10–50.
- IPIP Positive Expressivity, 9 items: positive positions 1–6, reverse 7–9; sum 9–45. The official page identifies a reduced nine-item form, not ten items.
- IPIP Negative Expressivity, 10 items: positive positions 1–5, reverse 6–10; sum 10–50. The last item, “Wish I could more easily show my negative feelings.”, is reverse-keyed exactly as published; it has not been changed to match an intuitive interpretation.

Exact items and signs: https://ipip.ori.org/newSingleConstructsKey.htm and https://ipip.ori.org/newEmotionalIntelligenceKey.htm. Frozen review fixture: ipip-five-source-proof.json, reviewed 2026-10-14. Exact full-set deduplication covers all prior definitions, including the first batch; overlapping individual public-domain IPIP items are permissible and do not imply duplicate scales.

https://ipip.ori.org/newSingleConstructs.htm reports adult Eugene-Springfield Community Sample evidence: IPIP alpha .71/.81/.72 and correlations with corresponding original control scales .53/.53/.62. These are sample-specific research results; the IPIP forms are not the original Levenson instruments. https://ipip.ori.org/newEmotionalIntelligenceTable.htm reports alpha .81/.79 for Positive Expressivity and .75/.74 for Negative Expressivity in women/men (N=432/317). It describes components potentially related to emotional intelligence, not an EI diagnosis or proficiency examination. No claim of pediatric, clinical, cross-cultural or Russian-language validity is made.

## Rights, administration and scoring

Public-domain commercial/non-commercial use: https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm. Online administration and implementation-quality caveat: https://ipip.ori.org/IPIPImplementationsAroundTheWeb.htm. The explanatory reports are original Holistic House educational text, not reproduced copyrighted interpretive manuals. Rights permission is not endorsement or clinical suitability.

General self-description, original English only; standard five IPIP accuracy anchors from https://ipip.ori.org/newScoringInstructions.htm. Positive answers score 1–5; negative answers score 6−x. All items required, no imputation or fabricated missingness allowance. The test suite independently checks every item against the frozen official-source strings and sign lists, every attainable sum, missing/out-of-range inputs, exact provenance and invalid dimensions.

All results are continuous, neutral descriptions; no percentiles, arbitrary bands, diagnosis or therapeutic targets. More expressivity is not necessarily better. Control-belief scores do not establish actual power, individual blame or a required worldview. Physical affection is optional and requires consent. No emotion score excuses harmful behavior.

## Implementation limitations

The versioned source definitions and scoring keys are in ipip-five-registration.json. Every exact definition must be verified before availability is enabled. Registration and code availability are separate checks; source documentation alone does not prove a live deployment. This implementation keeps these general self-descriptions out of automatic starter batteries and course score targets. No Russian questionnaire translation is provided.
