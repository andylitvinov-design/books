# Three original-English IPIP scales: review candidate

The added keys are ipip-cognitive-failures-10, ipip-optimism-10 and ipip-attending-to-emotions-10. Exact deduplication compares full item sets, not just similar construct names; optimism remains distinct from the existing four-item IPIP-VIA-R Hope scale.

## Source and rights review

The exact item strings, order and reversal positions are recorded in ipip-three-source-proof.json from the official IPIP scoring-key pages (reviewed 2026-10-14). Cognitive Failures: positive 1–6, reverse 7–10. Optimism: positive 1–4, reverse 5–10. Attending to Emotions: positive 1–5, reverse 6–10. The official response anchors and sum method come from https://ipip.ori.org/newScoringInstructions.htm. Sum range 10–50, reverse 6−x. Missing or malformed answers reject scoring.

https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm permits commercial and non-commercial use of public-domain IPIP items, scales and inventories. https://ipip.ori.org/IPIPImplementationsAroundTheWeb.htm explicitly allows online administration and cautions that permission does not guarantee third-party implementation quality.

https://ipip.ori.org/newSingleConstructs.htm distinguishes the 10-item IPIP Cognitive Failures form from the 25-item original, and the 10-item IPIP Optimism form from the six scored original items. Reported IPIP alpha values .79 and .86, respectively, are sample-specific research evidence, not certification of this site. The Attending to Emotions table https://ipip.ori.org/newEmotionalIntelligenceTable.htm reports ten items and alpha .81/.83 in women/men; this is one component potentially related to emotional intelligence, not a total EI or emotion-regulation score.

## Interpretation and course suitability

Prepared interpretation uses continuous sums, with exact provenance and dimension checks. No thresholds, normative percentiles, clinical severity or arbitrary score bands are added. All three scales are neutral in health classification and ineligible as skill/course score targets. Attending to emotions is explicitly not monotonically better with higher scores. These general self-descriptions have no automatic repeat interval or starter-battery enrollment. English instrument questions remain English in the Russian interface; no Russian instrument version is registered.

## Implementation limitations

The exact versioned source definitions are in ipip-three-registration.json. Source definitions, scoring configuration and online availability must remain consistent. Verify the exact version before enabling it; code presence alone is not evidence of a live deployment. English instrument questions remain English in both interface languages. No Russian instrument version, clinical score target or automatic retest interval is included.
