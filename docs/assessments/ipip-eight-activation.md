# Eight source-backed IPIP scales

## Included forms

- Cognitive Failures, 10 items
- Optimism, 10 items
- Attending to Emotions, 10 items
- Locus of Control: Internality, 10 items
- Locus of Control: Powerful Others, 10 items
- Locus of Control: Chance, 10 items
- Positive Expressivity, 9 items
- Negative Expressivity, 10 items

The exact English items, keys and source references are documented in ipip-three-source-proof.json and ipip-five-source-proof.json and their individual guides. Immutable definitions remain original-en-v1; enabling availability does not change their content hashes.

## Scoring and interpretation

Answers use the official five accuracy anchors. Positive items score 1–5, reverse items use 6 minus the answer, and all items are summed. Positive Expressivity ranges 9–45; the seven ten-item forms range 10–50. All answers are required, with no imputation.

Only continuous descriptive results are produced. No diagnostic categories, invented cutoffs, normative percentiles or automatic health rankings are added. More emotional attention or expression is not necessarily better. Control-belief scores do not establish actual power or blame. Physical affection is optional and requires consent. Scores are not course improvement targets.

## Implementation limitations

Exact version identity, content and scoring must be verified before enabling availability. A source definition or a draft code change alone does not demonstrate live deployment. The fail-closed availability guard remains for future prepared versions. All eight original-English questionnaires remain English in EN/RU interfaces; translated interface text is not a validated questionnaire translation. No automatic repeat interval or starter-battery enrollment is added.

Verification includes immutable source parity, every attainable sum, required and malformed answers, provenance tampering, bilingual interface discovery and reports, and synthetic lifecycle persistence. Local database lifecycle checks do not replace browser, HTTP endpoint, role-policy, concurrency or production acceptance testing.
