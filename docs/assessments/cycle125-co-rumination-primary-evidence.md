## Edition and legal cautions

- Research translation and within-sample reliability are not the same as validated scale equivalence, commercial redistribution rights, or transferable clinical norms.
- The Italian row quotes the 27-item *parent form*, not an independently verified Italian retained-item count. The Quebec row is a study translation with sample reliability; treat full independent language validation as unresolved. PANCRS study participant location does not establish item language.
- Compare using stable key **plus population + form/version + respondent language + source DOI**. CRQ-27 and contextual CRQ-16/CRQ-9 forms are not interchangeable; factor structures and scoring may differ.
- Spanish adolescent CRQ-9 has study-specific longitudinal psychometric evidence; no new independently validated Russian CRQ/PANCRS edition found in this review.
- Permission to translate granted to other researchers is **not** permission for Holistic House to host commercial respondent items or automated clinical interpretation.
