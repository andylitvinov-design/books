## Instrument licensing and safety
New records are conservatively **research-only** and `enabled: false`:
- 17 `rights_review`;
- 116 `permission_required`;
- 38 `licensed_only`;
- 61 `clinician_only`.

These categories are **release gates**, not individual legal determinations. The source URLs supplied for these entries are **catalogue discovery starting points**, not instrument-specific commercial licences or proof that every measure is listed by every indexed site. Each version needs owner/manual provenance, approved language, complete items and scorer, clinician/safety review (where applicable), appropriately restricted consent, and quality assurance before moving into `MONITORING_CATALOG` or `app.assessment_versions`.

Do not convert a protected test into a supposed original just by rephrasing its copyrighted items, preserving its proprietary scoring algorithm, or using its protected name. An independently authored educational questionnaire may use broad uncopyrightable constructs but must not claim the original instrument's validation, norms or equivalent scores. For children, suicidality, trauma, dissociation, personality pathology, psychosis and neuropsychological measures, require qualified oversight and separate safety handling. Do not apply psychotherapy results as diagnostics or directly map them to homeopathic prescriptions.

## Research landscape / competitors
Discovery references (not universal permission grants):
- [APA PsycTests](https://www.apa.org/pubs/databases/psyctests) — broad indexed repository of psychological measures.
- [NovoPsych Assessments](https://novopsych.com/assessments/) — competitor instrument catalogue, scoring and progress tracking.
- [Psychology Tools](https://www.psychologytools.com/professional/assessment/) — clinical assessment directory and clinician tools.
- [IPIP](https://ipip.ori.org/) — public-domain personality-item pools; licensing details [here](https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm); official keys and item selection must be verified.
- [NHS Talking Therapies](https://www.england.nhs.uk/mental-health/adults/nhs-talking-therapies/) — routinely administered outcome instruments.
- [VA National Center for PTSD](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/assessment/adult-sr/index.asp) — official PTSD measure summaries.
- [HealthMeasures / PROMIS](https://www.healthmeasures.net/explore-measurement-systems/promis) — official domain measurement system and administration/licensing.
- [CORE System Trust](https://www.coresystemtrust.org.uk/home/instruments/) — CORE outcome questionnaires and use conditions.
- [Mind Garden IIP](https://www.mindgarden.com/113-inventory-of-interpersonal-problems) — proprietary interpersonal problems scales.

No claim of a finite global universe: research/publisher databases regularly add editions and specialty measures.
