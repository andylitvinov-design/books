# Nonattachment scales — research notes

Nonattachment Scale (NAS): 30, 7 and 8 items; a Rasch study examined 26 retained items.
Nonattachment to Self Scale (NTS): distinct 7-item instrument.
Both remain research only pending digital commercial-use permission.
Sources: https://doi.org/10.1080/00223890903425960 and https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02544
