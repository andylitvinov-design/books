TANTRA REIKI — DETAILED COURSE OVERVIEW
=======================================================

A nine-stage programme of personal, body-aware, symbolic and meditative practices. The themes below follow the author's original stage descriptions. Attunement names refer to the tradition's symbolic practices, not guaranteed effects.

Level 1. Spring of Life — Awakening sensitivity
- The first step invites you to experience the Tantra Reiki flow through the body: gentle sensations, pleasure, vitality and a more present connection with yourself and a partner.
Attunements: Sexual energy activation; Attractiveness · qualities of charisma; Situation activation
Suggested practice: Learn to notice the flow and practise respectfully with a partner.

Level 2. Fire of Life — Energy, strength and confidence
- The second level explores the image of an inner fire. The original course connects this step with gathering energy, feeling more personally present and working symbolically with limitations and material goals.
Attunements: Accumulation of energy; Money Magnet; Burn Away Complexes
Suggested practice: Create your first simple Tantra Reiki mandala and explore symbolic exercises for personal focus and confidence.

Level 3. Ocean of Unity — Attunement with the world
- The image of this level is the ocean: moving beyond the feeling of being separate and learning to notice connection with a larger field. It introduces the themes of unity, clearing and favourable movement.
Attunements: Attunement; Luck · acceleration of time; Talisman
Suggested practice: Reflect on your experiences, practise attunement with a partner, and develop a Tantra Reiki mandala.

Level 4. The Vertical of Love — Archetypes and connection
- This level turns to the image of a vertical connection with the worlds of archetypes and the Gods of Love. The Astral Child is described in the tradition as a symbolic shared field of a couple or group.
Attunements: Enlightenment; Gods of Love; Astral Child · shared couple or group field
Suggested practice: Begin supervised work on Tantra Reiki mandalas for individual requests.

Level 5. Inner Light — The source within
- Instead of imagining a current arriving from outside, the fifth level explores light arising from within. Its themes are creativity, personal energy and the symbolic opening of energy centres.
Attunements: Inner Light
Suggested practice: Explore charging symbolic Tantra Reiki artifacts with personal supervision.

Level 6. Worlds of Unity — Rest and integration
- The sixth level uses the imagery of deep stillness and eternity. The original description speaks of calm, inner support, balance and the experience of being part of a greater whole.
Attunements: Worlds of Unity
Suggested practice: Design a clear outline for a personal Tantra Reiki practice session.

Level 7. Illumination — Clarity of attention
- The seventh level shifts toward the experience of a clearer and more focused awareness. In the tradition, attention itself becomes central to the work of harmonising experience.
Attunements: Illumination
Suggested practice: Practise with feedback, document observations and collect testimonials only with consent.

Level 8. Creation of the World — The creative impulse
- This stage explores a move from merely sensing or harmonising the flow to actively shaping creative intentions. The original text describes it as an impulse of creation.
Attunements: Creation of the World
Suggested practice: Create a personal symbolic ritual and write an educational reflection or article.

Level 9. Fullness of Unity — Integration and mastery
- The ninth level brings together the preceding images and practices. Its central idea is the experience of inner fullness, strength, balance and a unified perspective.
Attunements: Fullness of Unity
Suggested practice: Master-level initiation and final assessment are described in the historical programme; current certification terms should be confirmed directly.

Course timing, format, eligibility and availability are confirmed individually by the teacher. This is educational and spiritual content, not medical advice, treatment or a promise of outcomes.
