REIKI YGGDRASIL — DETAILED COURSE OVERVIEW
=======================================================

The seven-module Reiki Yggdrasil curriculum begins with five foundational levels and continues through the Instructor Course and symbolic studies of temple traditions, Eastern and Western schools, runes and advanced practice.

Module 1. Basic Reiki Yggdrasil Program
Focus: The first five levels introduce the school's vocabulary of intuitive perception, personal protection, cleansing, resources, inner strength and the symbolic master pathway.
  1. Health · Intuition · Protection
  2. Cleansing · Money Activation
  3. Predestination · Power
  4. Extrasensory Vision
  5. Master Level

Module 2. Instructor Course
Focus: The instructor curriculum expands into reflective practices around support, relationships, vitality, embodiment and focused management of attention and energy imagery.
  1. Healing
  2. Golden Calf
  3. Man and Woman
  4. Life Force
  5. Sexual Energy
  6. Fireball · Energy Management

Module 3. Temple Magic
Focus: Temple traditions are approached through cultural and archetypal images: Egyptian and Greek mythology, Toltec themes, Sufism and the concept of collective symbolic fields.
  1. Working with Egregores
  2. Egyptian Magic
  3. Greek Magic · Zodiac
  4. Toltec Magic
  5. Sufism

Module 4. Eastern Magic
Focus: Eastern studies examine the five-element model, I Ching symbolism, Kundalini imagery and historical concepts associated with intention and material resources.
  1. Chinese Medicine 1 · Elements
  2. Chinese Medicine 2 · Power
  3. Chinese Divination · I Ching
  4. Kundalini
  5. Money Magic

Module 5. Western European Magic · Kabbalah & Tarot
Focus: Western European themes include Tarot, the symbolic powers of the elements, the Tree of Sephiroth and contemplative approaches to divination.
  1. Major Arcana of Tarot
  2. Powers of the Elements
  3. Tree of Sephiroth
  4. Higher Arcana of Tarot
  5. Tarot Divination

Module 6. Advanced Rune Magic
Focus: Runic study introduces symbols and mythic worlds of the Yggdrasil Tree, the Circle of Power, and the runic divination and healing traditions described in the syllabus.
  1. Runes & Runic Tradition
  2. Worlds of the Yggdrasil Tree
  3. Circle of Power
  4. Runic Divination
  5. Runic Healing

Module 7. High Magic
Focus: The final module surveys advanced mythic themes such as astral travel, symbolic helpers, Slavic traditions and imagined civilizations. Treat these as educational imagery, not factual abilities.
  1. Teleport · Astral Flight · Clairvoyance
  2. Machine Hall · Strengthening Vision
  3. Ifrits · Creating Helpers
  4. Slavic Magic 1
  5. Slavic Magic 2
  6. Civilizations

Course timing, format, eligibility and availability are confirmed individually by the teacher. This is educational and spiritual content, not medical advice, treatment or a promise of outcomes.
