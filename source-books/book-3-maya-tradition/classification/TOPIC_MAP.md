# Topic map and knowledge-level classification

Topics are derived from the captured wording, titles, named images, and explicit source vocabulary. A label identifies the type of material asserted or performed in a post; it does not validate a claim. “Historical fact” is deliberately marked as a **source assertion**, while ritual, mythic, authorial, therapeutic, and hypothetical material remains distinct.

## Editorial knowledge-level taxonomy

- **Historical fact (source assertion):** a claim about a past people, object, place, date, or practice; it remains unverified until fact-checked.
- **Archaeological interpretation:** an inference about an artifact, site, image, or material remain. No post is elevated to this level solely because it shows an object; the source must make an interpretive claim.
- **Mythology:** material framed through deities, cosmological figures, or named mythic places.
- **Traditional material:** material that the source attributes to ritual, shamanic, calendrical, or community practice.
- **Modern reconstruction:** a contemporary reconstruction explicitly identified as such. No source text is assigned this label without that identification.
- **Author interpretation:** the author's own channel, energy, practice, or experiential reading.
- **Archetypal/therapeutic model:** the author's therapeutic or archetypal framing.
- **Hypothesis:** wording marked by uncertainty, possibility, or a question.

Posts can carry more than one label. The table does not convert a source assertion into verified history.
