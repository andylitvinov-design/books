# Fact-check queue — do not rewrite the source

This file records source claims that need external verification before a public historical or educational edition. It does not correct, replace, or add claims. Authorial exercises, metaphysical language, and therapeutic models remain attributed to the author; their inclusion here is not endorsement.

## Priority rules

1. Check proper names, transliterations, deity identifications, and claimed translations.
2. Check dates, sites, artifact descriptions, and statements about Maya/Aztec/Zapotec/Inca practice.
3. Distinguish archaeological interpretation, mythology, traditional material, modern reconstruction, author interpretation, archetypal/therapeutic model, and hypothesis in any published apparatus.
4. Preserve a clear source citation even where a claim is unsupported or cannot be verified.
