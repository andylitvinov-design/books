BACH OAK Remedy

Has a deep stabilizing and supportive effect.

Suitable for strong, responsible, and resilient people who are used to bearing the load and not stopping, even when they are running out of strength.

The remedy gives a sense of internal restoration and support.

A person remains strong and reliable but moves away from a state of exhausting struggle.

ESSENCE OF THE REMEDY

OAK — is oak.

Oak is a symbol of strength, resilience, and endurance.

It withstands winds, storms, and harsh conditions, continuing to stand and grow.

It is the archetype of a warrior who never gives up.

MEANING

OAK reflects a state of constant struggle and tension.

When a person:

feels enormous responsibility
is used to handling everything by themselves
continues working even when exhausted
does not allow themselves to rest
considers stopping a weakness.

Internally, there is a mindset:

"I must endure"

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

8–12 years — stage of responsibility and role

in the team.

This is the age when a child begins to feel:

duty

obligation

the need to be strong

the expectations of others.

If at this time the mindset is formed

"I must be strong and not let anyone down,"

a person may begin to live in a state of constant tension.

CORE OF THE TRAUMA

"I do not have the right to stop."

"If I weaken, everything will collapse."

"I must endure."

"I am obliged to be strong."

Inside accumulates:

chronic tension

fatigue

suppression of one's own needs

constant mobilization.

## EFFECT

The medication gently:

reduces inner fighting tension

restores connection with resources

allows rest without guilt

recovers vital energy

restores the natural balance of effort and rest.

Strength remains, but becomes alive and resilient.

IDEA OF THE MEDICATION

True strength is not in constant struggle,

but in the harmony of strength and recovery.

Even an oak needs

time to rest in order to grow.

MESSAGE OF THE PREPARATION

strength does not require constant tension

rest is part of strength

life supports you

you can be strong and gentle at the same time

resilience is born from balance.

TRANSFORMATION IMAGE

An old oak stands in the field.

For a long time, it resisted storms,
straining every branch.

But gradually the wind begins to gently sway the crown.

The oak no longer fights the wind —
it moves with it.

Its roots are deep,
and strength becomes calm.

## RITUAL

Take 2–5 drops of OAK under the tongue,
place your hand on the solar plexus,

imagine heavy armor on your body —
armor of constant struggle.

With each exhale, the armor begins to slowly dissolve.

Beneath it appears a strong and living body.

Imagine yourself standing like an oak —
with deep roots and a calm crown.

Say the phrases:

- My strength is calm and deep
- I am allowed to rest
- Life supports me

- I am stable and free

- my strength is restoring. 🌳
