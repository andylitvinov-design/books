ROCK ROSE Remedy

ESSENCE

This is the energy of the rock rose.

A small plant that grows in harsh conditions — on stones, under the sun and wind.

Fragile on the outside,

but inside — pure life force and instant mobilization.

MEANING

This is the energy of overcoming intense fear.

It is the energy that helps

move from a state of panic and terror to a state of action.

This remedy activates the inner hero

at the moment when everything inside tightens.

ROCK ROSE is suitable

When a person:

• experiences intense fear or panic
• feels horror, shock
• loses control in a stressful situation
• feels paralysis or extreme tension
• goes through acute crises

The remedy provides instant mobilization and clarity.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

Period of basic safety (early stages, including prenatal experience).

This is the stage when the sense of:

• whether it is safe to live
• whether the world can be trusted

is formed.

If it arises

strong fear,

a state is formed:

"the world is dangerous, I am in danger"

TRAUMA CORE

"I am in danger."

"I may not survive."

"I can't cope."

## EFFECT

The remedy gently but quickly:

• relieves panic state

• restores control

• activates inner strength

• brings clarity and action

A feeling arises:

"I am composed and can act"

MESSAGE OF THE REMEDY

• fear can be overcome

• there is strength within

• even in a crisis, there is support

• you are capable of action

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

A scale.

A small flower grows on it.

Strong wind, storm, tension.

It seems it won't hold.

But the flower does not break.

It opens toward the sun.

And in this moment is revealed

pure life force.

## RITUAL

• take 2–5 drops of ROCK ROSE under the tongue

• place your hand on your chest

• imagine strong tension inside

• with each breath, light enters the body

• the tension begins to expand

• strength appears inside and

clarity

Say the phrases:

• I am safe

• I am composed

• I can act

• I have strength

Experience shows that the medication provides an instant return to stability.

A person is struck by lightning - a wave of awakening.

Panic and paralysis disappear, a state appears:

“I am here, I am alive, I can act”.
