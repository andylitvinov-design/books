Avena Sativa (oat energy).

This is another remedy that captivated me.

Working with clients, I liked how it gives the momentum to move forward.

It brings enthusiasm, interest, the desire to create something.

In it is the sweet power of a sprouting seed.

Avena Sativa —

used to restore energy, relieve fatigue, and increase tone, motivation, and libido.

## INDICATIONS

- fatigue, overwork, loss of tone, appetite loss, decreased sexual desire, problems with concentration, scattered attention.

BASE: Oat (Avena sativa).

- symbolizes nourishment, recovery, and strengthening of vital forces.

PERIOD: CARE (1.5-3 years).

- here the sense of warmth, contact, and self-care is developed.

## EFFECT

- restores strength, motivation, interest.

## IMAGE

- A tired traveler at the edge of a field inhales the scent of oats, drawing strength to continue the journey,

- an exhausted field after a long drought,

which needs recovery and nourishment for a new growth cycle,

- an oat plant stretching towards the sun, symbolizing the revival of energy.

## ARCHETYPE

- Source of strength

- Healer of exhaustion.

- Renewal of power.

## ARCHETYPE IDEA

- Revival of energy through self-care.

9. ARCHETYPE LESSON:

- Resources need to be replenished in time to avoid exhaustion.

10. ARCHETYPE SUPPORT:

- Strengthening resources, restoring stability.

- Provides energy to gain joy in life.

- Helps to slow down to feel alive again.

11. Shadow (to accept):

- weakness, inability to move due to exhaustion.

- helplessness, loss of contact with inner resources.

- difficulty in taking care of oneself.

12. RESOURCE (to reveal):

- power of renewal

- unfolding of inner resources.

## TRANSFORMATION

- from exhaustion and weakness to strength and vitality.

- from apathy and burnout to joy in life,

motivation to act.

- from helplessness to confidence, regaining control over your life.

Process: The remedy helps to gently go through the path from complete exhaustion to fullness, connecting body and soul to create a resource for a new stage of life.

PS. The energies of this remedy remind me of the Mysteries of Yarilya / Kolyada / Ivan Kupala.

This remedy plants a spark within you to ignite the flame of desires.
