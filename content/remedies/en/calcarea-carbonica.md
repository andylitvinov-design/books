Calcarea Carbonica - adjustment.

In a shell quiet, like in a warmed house

I hide from the world, from the fears in my chest.

But the world lures me with flowers over the hill.

Inviting me: "You can! Go!"

A sprout breaks through obstacles,

Fragile but strong, it reaches upwards.

In my soul, waterfalls are already murmuring

And fear disappears. I believe. I exist.

Fear of change, shadow of loneliness—

You are my protection, but also chains.

The world over there, beyond the border, calls every day,

Glimmering: "Grow! I am with you. Be ready."

Calcarea will advise: "Trust yourself,

For your strength is in every step and breath.

Do not fear falls, do not fear losses—

In them, life reveals its care for us."

Now I go, cautiously but firmly,

Letting reality in with radiance in my chest.

My shell is support,

Not a net, not chains.

I bravely face the turns of the road.
