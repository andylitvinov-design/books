### message601

KALI MURIATICUM

Another remedy that gently fills a closed heart with love.

It removes blocks of resentment and depression. Adds freshness and tenderness to the soul.

BASIS

Potassium chloride - helps transmit nerve impulses in muscles and regulate the balance of salts in fluids.

Symbolizes the ability to relieve inflammation and stagnation.

Helps build connections and achieve harmony in life.

INDICATIONS:

- hidden grievances inside

- shame

- fear of speaking out

- fear of being wrong

- fear of conflict

- stagnation, emotional and physical

- inflammation, swelling

EFFECT:

- release of emotions and grievances

- lightness, easier breathing

- resorption of stagnation

- return movement to life, body

ARCHETYPE

- silent keeper of pain

- discreet and correct

- dead voice of the soul (fear of speaking out)

LOOK:

- A frozen vessel in which the mucus of emotions and understatement accumulates

- A foggy mirror that reflects but does not transmit light.

- Standing water in a swamp

- Knot in the throat

CONFLICT: 5-8

years.

Self-worth stage.

If I tell the truth, I will lose love.

IDEA

Stagnation is when you don’t allow yourself to speak out.

When you allow yourself to feel and speak, you give back life.

MESSAGE

You don't have to endure everything inside.

Your voice matters.

You have the right to say.

TRANSFORMATION:

- Before: stagnation, silence, accumulated grievances, internal “fog”

- After: lightness, freedom of voice, movement of emotions, freshness of life.

RITUAL

Place your hands on your chest. Imagine how the mucus in your chest dissolves in the light.

As you exhale, begin to sound.
