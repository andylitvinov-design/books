KALI MURIATICUM

Another remedy that gently fills the closed heart with love.

It removes blocks of resentment, depression. Adds freshness and tenderness to the soul.

## BASE

Potassium chloride - helps transmit nerve impulses in muscles and regulate the balance of salts in fluids.

Symbolizes the ability to reduce inflammation, stagnation.

Helps build connections and achieve harmony in life.

## INDICATIONS

- hidden resentments inside

- shame

- fear of speaking out

- fear of being wrong

- fear of conflict

- stagnation, emotional and physical

- inflammation, swelling

## EFFECT

- release of emotions and resentment

- lightness, ease of breathing

- dissolution of stagnation

- return of movement to life, body

## ARCHETYPE

- silent keeper of pain

- restrained and proper

- muted voice of the soul (fear of speaking out)

## IMAGE

- Frozen vessel in which mucus of emotions and unspoken words accumulates

- Foggy mirror,

which reflects but does not let light through.

- Standing water in a swamp

- Lump in the throat

CONFLICT: 5-8 years old.

Stage of self-worth.

If I tell the truth, I will lose love.

## IDEA

Stagnation - when you do not allow yourself to speak out.

When you allow yourself to feel and speak, you bring life back.

## MESSAGE

You are not obliged to endure everything inside.

Your voice is important.

You have the right to speak.

## TRANSFORMATION

- Before: stagnation, silence, accumulated resentments, inner 'fog'

- After: lightness, liberation of voice, movement of emotions, freshness of life.

## RITUAL

Place your hands on your chest. Imagine the mucus in your chest dissolving in the light.

Exhale and begin to sound.
