HYDRASTIS CANADENSIS

(Hydrastis, Canadian Goldenrod)

A very curious preparation.

The first effect of the preparation, according to experience, is the emergence of courage, boldness, youthful self-confidence.

You don't care about anything, you carry yourself as you are. It's unusual and intoxicating.

And the essence, the main effect of the preparation, is that it cleanses the mucus from the soul. It removes accumulated grievances, shame, guilt, weakness. It helps in early stress situations, when there were not enough tears to cleanse and strength to live anew.

📌 BASIS

Hydrastis is a plant with a bitter, astringent taste.

Physically, it is used as a mucus-cleansing, anti-inflammatory, immune-stimulating agent.

Homeopathically, it is one of the main remedies for chronic weakness, depressions with guilt and emptiness.

🧱 STAGES OF DEVELOPMENT

- Care (1.5–3 years) — used to being “independent,” not bothering anyone, taking care of others, but not receiving care oneself.

- Will and guilt (2–4 years) — suppression of desire

to ask or to take.

- Conflicts:

A person feels tired, heavy, sluggish

* "I am tired, but I can't quit"

* "I am alone, and no one will support me"

* "I feel like I am rotting, but I don't even have the strength to ask for help."

Hydrastis often even affects the very beginning — the "right to exist," the Birth phase, and at the same time — restores the inner center, core, related to the theme of contact, purity, and inner authority.

🔥 INDICATIONS

- feeling of emptiness,

- weakness of the personal center,

- decreased mental clarity and immune defense,

- the person is emotionally poisoned by the past, cannot cleanse themselves.

- there is depression with passivity, mental exhaustion, slow thinking.

- inflammation of mucous membranes

- fatigue, apathy,

- depression with an element of resentment or 'souring'

- sluggishness, self-disgust, feeling old, irrelevant, useless

- heaviness in the body, especially in the abdomen,

head, chest

- mental fog, inability to concentrate.

🧱 ARCHETYPES

🔸 Old man with a bitter soul — knows too much pain to believe in joy

🔸 Wise man, tired of suffering — lives automatically

🔸 Forgotten mother — carries in her body the burden of resentment, fatigue, and self-denial

🌿 IMAGES

- Stagnant water — does not move, does not breathe, does not renew

- Old woman washing hands, but the dirt doesn't go away

- Mirror covered with film, in which the face cannot be seen.

💡 IDEA OF THE REMEDY

- To come alive — you don't need to be strong.

- You just need to wash away the old, give yourself room for light.

- Cleansing comes not through effort, but through gentleness and letting go.

🌺 AFFIRMATIONS

- I allow myself to be cleansed and gather myself in pieces.

- I return to the center of my strength.

- I am worthy of inner order, clarity, and purity.

- I let go of what is no longer needed

- My pain is cleansed, does not define me

- I

I allow myself light

- I cleanse and come alive.

🔄 TRANSFORMATION

Before: soul in slime, apathy, self-loathing, pain from the unexpressed.

After: clarity, softness, light cleansing, inner breathing, vitality.

🔮 RITUAL: "Washing Away"

Imagine that you are standing under a light, warm rain.

It gently washes off from you the sticky coating — the burdens, pain, words that you have carried for too long.

You do not toil, you do not cleanse by effort.

You just stand. The rain does the work for you.

And somewhere beneath the layer of fatigue — your face. It is fresh, calm. You are.
