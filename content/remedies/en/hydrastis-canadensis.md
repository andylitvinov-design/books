### message681

HYDRASTIS CANADENSIS

(Hydrastis, Goldenrod)

A very interesting remedy.

From experience, the first effect of the remedy is courage, courage, and youthful self-confidence.

You don’t care, you carry yourself as you are. It's unusual and intoxicating.

And the essence, the main effect of the remedy is that it cleanses the mucus from the soul. Removes the rubble of grievances, shame, guilt, weakness. It helps in early stress, when there were not enough tears to cleanse yourself and the strength to live again.

📌 BASIS

Hydrastis is a plant with a bitter, astringent taste.

Physically used as a mucus cleanser, anti-inflammatory, immunostimulating agent.

Homeopathically - one of the main remedies for chronic weakness, depression with guilt and devastation.

🧱 STAGES OF DEVELOPMENT

- Caring (1.5–3 years) - used to being “independent”, not bothering anyone, caring for others, but not receiving care himself.

- Will and guilt (2–4 years) - suppression of the desire to ask or take.

- Conflicts:

The person feels tired, heavy,

inhibited

* “I'm tired, but I can't quit”

* “I am alone and no one will support me”

* “I feel like I’m rotting, but I don’t even have the strength to ask for help.”

Hydrastis often even touches the very beginning - the "right to exist", the Birth phase, and at the same time - restores the inner center, the core associated with the theme of contact, purity, and inner authority.

🔥 INDICATIONS

- feeling of devastation,

- weakness of the personality center,

- decreased clarity of consciousness and immune defense

- a person is emotionally poisoned by the past and cannot cleanse himself.

- there is depression with passivity, mental exhaustion, slow thinking.

- inflammation of the mucous membranes

- fatigue, apathy,

- depression with an element of resentment or “sourness”

- stringiness, self-loathing, feeling old, irrelevant, useless

- heaviness in the body, especially in the stomach, head, chest

- mental fog, inability to concentrate.

🧱 ARCHETYPES

🔸 An old man with a bitter soul knows too much pain to believe in joy

🔸

A sage, tired of suffering, lives automatically

🔸 Forgotten mother - carries a load of grievances, fatigue and self-denial in her body

🌿 LOOKS

- Stale water - does not move, does not breathe, does not renew itself

- Old woman washing her hands, but the dirt doesn’t come away

- A mirror covered with plaque, in which the face is not visible.

💡 remedy IDEA

- You don’t need to be strong to come to life.

- You just need to wash away the old, give yourself a place for light.

- Purification comes not through effort, but through softness and letting go.

🌺 AFFIRMATIONS

- I allow myself to be cleansed and collect myself piece by piece.

- I return to the center of my power.

- I am worthy of internal order, clarity and purity.

- I let go of what is no longer needed

- My pain is cleared, does not define me

- I will allow myself the light

- I am cleansed and revived.

🔄 TRANSFORMATION

Before: soul in mucus, apathy, self-loathing, pain from the unexpressed.

After: clarity, softness, light cleansing, inner breathing, vitality.

🔮 RITUAL: “Washing away”

Imagine that you are standing under a light, warm

rain.

He gently washes away the viscous coating from you - the burden, the pain, the words that you have been carrying for too long.

You don't work, you don't purify yourself with effort.

You're just standing there. The rain does the work for you.

And somewhere under the layer of fatigue is your face. It is fresh, calm. You exist.
