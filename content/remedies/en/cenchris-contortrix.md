CENCHRIS CONTORTRIX (Copperhead Snake)

This remedy is like the awakening of a volcano.

It triggers fiery energy within, which fills, saturates.

This is a remedy for people who crave contact, passion, love, but at the same time fear their wildness, aggression, and sexuality.

When there is a desire for attention and expression, but it is frightening.

The remedy awakens strength and dignity.

A strong remedy.

I use it to activate personal strength, courage, decisiveness, when there is fear or constraint.

🌿 Base

Copperhead snake venom. A snake bite is accompanied by burning pain, a feeling of pressure, a rush of blood, and heightened body sensitivity.

Symbolically — passion, temptation, pleasure, but with fear of self-destruction.

💥 Indications

- In bursts of passion, attraction, and guilt for one's desires.

- Alternation of passion and emptiness

- Periods of anger, jealousy, irritation, and feeling unnecessary

- A mixture

passions and disappointments.

- Craving for love, attention, a touch of approval, with suffering

- Feeling lonely in a couple: I'm with someone – but I'm lonely

- Attraction to dangerous partners, desire for seduction

- Feeling of self-hatred for being "needy"

- Hypersexuality with fear of losing control.

- Diseases with congestion in the pelvis, pain, a feeling of stagnant energy.

🔥 Archetypes

- Poisonous Princess - Seduces, but remains empty

- A lonely beauty who is torn apart by longing for love

- A priestess of seduction who has lost the balance between desire and acceptance

- A wounded Goddess of Love, with an inner pain of unrecognized value

- A female temptress who plays with the fire of her own sexuality.

- A woman who dances with the fire of desire, who experiences attraction and fear of it.

🌸 remedy EFFECT

- Helps to relax stiff passion, turn it into warm femininity/

masculinity

- Removes the burning pain of loneliness

- Helps to free from the obsession with desire, dependence on approval

- Restores contact with oneself, with inner peace

- Relieves the 'never satisfied love' syndrome

- Helps to feel that love begins from within

- Helps to find a golden mean between passion and clarity

- Relieves hyperarousal

- Helps to cleanse sexual shame

🧱 Stage of development

0.1–1.5 years — deficit of basic physical contact, feeling of 'I was not held'

3–6 years — right to love, acceptance of one's sexuality, the right to be desired, shame for sensuality

5–8 years — Self-esteem and control: seeking recognition through seductiveness, fear of being worthless without attractiveness

TASK:

reveal sensuality without self-destruction

🖼 Images

- A woman with red hair in red garments, dancing in warm light

- A red snake coiling

body flowing into the heart.

- A warm female body in the rays of the setting sun, filled with the power of life.

💡 Concept of the product

Love begins from within.

You are worthy of love not because you seduce someone, but because you exist.

You can be passionate without losing yourself.

Sexuality is not a weapon, but the radiance of your life.

⚫ Shadow / conflict

"I either captivate or I am worthless."

"I am loved only for my passion."

📖 Message / lesson

Your sexuality is part of your wholeness.

You can enjoy yourself without fearing rejection.

You have the right to live with pleasure, without the fear of losing yourself.

🌺 Affirmations

I allow myself to enjoy.

My body is a temple of joy, not of shame.

I am whole, regardless of desire.

My sexuality is a source of life, not of pain.

I am radiant and free.

🔄 Transformation

Before: hyperexcitement, passionate outbursts, feeling of emptiness after pleasure.

After: whole

energy, sexuality connected with self-love.

🖌 IMAGE / MEDITATION

Imagine yourself standing in front of a mirror.

You see your body — and for the first time feel not shame, but warmth.

You touch yourself with love.

You smile because love is born from within.

You don't have to wait for love from outside — you already carry it.

🔮 RITUAL

Light a candle. Caress your skin, hug yourself.

Say: "I am whole. I am loved. I fill myself with love. I am a woman with a soft heart and a warm body. I deserve affection — first of all from myself."

Feel yourself filled from within. You are beautiful in your strength. And free.
