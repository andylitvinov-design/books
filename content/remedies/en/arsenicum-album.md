ARSENICUM ALBUM

One of the basic remedies.

It treats states of unexplained anxiety, when there is a need to keep everything under control. What if everything falls apart.

Usually, this indicates that the psyche is not coping sufficiently yet. The need to keep life under control consumes a huge amount of personal resources. And the remedy helps resolve this situation.

## INDICATIONS

Feeling weak.

Fear, anxiety, need for control and protection from chaos.

Anxiety that worsens when alone and requires the presence of others.

## BASE

Arsenic. Causes weakness, anxiety, feeling vulnerable, need for protection.

## EFFECT

Transformation of fear into calmness and confidence.

## IMAGE

- A person standing on the edge of a cliff, trembling with fear and desperately trying to keep control over their life, afraid of losing support and being left alone with chaos.

- A home, carefully cleaned and organized, but inside

in whom a feeling of anxiety and fear lives.

## ARCHETYPE

- Anxious Controller.

## IDEA

- The struggle between anxiety and trust.

## SHADOW OF THE ARCHETYPE

- Obsessive need to control oneself and the surrounding world.

- Fear of losing control, stability, trust, and safety.

- Inability to relax.

- Perfectionism and egocentricity.

## RESOURCE

- Confidence even in uncertainty,

- Discipline that allows one to cope with difficulties,

- Ability to create order,

- Caring for oneself and others through constructive means.

## LESSON

- Developing trust in others and the world

- Accepting chaos and changes as part of life

- Discovering one’s vulnerability as a source of strength and development

- Freedom from the need for security and entering a world of opportunities.

## INNER CONFLICT

- Desire for complete order and helplessness in achieving it

- Desire to be active - but internal

exhaustion

- need for support - but fear of becoming dependent

- desire for isolation for control, but fear of loneliness

ARSENICUM ALBUM

- This remedy helps to cope with deep fears, restores a sense of security and harmony, teaches to accept uncertainty and find inner peace.
