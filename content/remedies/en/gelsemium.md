GELSEMIUM

A remedy valuable in situations of weakness, fear, physical and emotional exhaustion.

EXAMPLE.

Working with a client, with a request regarding study overloads, difficulty completing tasks.

The remedy Gelsemium brings one out of stupor, creating a desire to move forward, act, and express oneself.

BASIS for the remedy: yellowroot – a plant that causes tremors, weakness, drowsiness, reflecting a state of fear and paralysis.

The plant reduces the transmission of impulses.

Symptoms: tremor, weakness, stupor, freezing, muscle hypotonia, cold, tremor.

USED FOR

relieving the state of "frozenness" before stress.

The remedy eliminates tremors, physical tension, emotional overload (apathy, fatigue, a sense of helplessness).

SYMBOL

A moment of pause, freezing before an important leap.

## ARCHETYPE

- anxious frozen

- numb

- paralyzed will

THE REMEDY IS RELEVANT

In states of stupor, exhaustion,

when support is needed for decisive actions.

## ALCHEMY

Acts as a catalyst, relieves fear, passivity, stagnation, retreat into oneself — awakens clarity and determination.

## TRANSFORMATION

* BEFORE: "Paralyzed by fear."

- Belief: If I show myself or make a mistake, there will be shame or danger.

- Fear: Public mistakes, failure, judgment.

- Behavior: Freezing, avoiding responsibility, postponing actions, physical tremors or weakness.

* AFTER: "Acting despite fear."

- Belief: Fear is a natural part of growth.

- Strategy: I can act even if I feel anxious.

- Behavior: Confidence appears in performances, decision-making, and the ability to take responsibility.

EXIT MODEL:

- The body under stress learns:

"I don't have to freeze in response to stress.

I can choose gentle or active action."
