BACH VINE Remedy.

Has a rather pronounced effect.

Suitable for strong, willful people who control everything, but at the same time their life force and energy begin to contract and melt away.

Experience shows that the remedy gives a sigh of relief. The grip remains, but without excessive tension. And the energy inside begins to recover.

ESSENCE OF THE REMEDY

VINE is not wine, as it may seem. It is the energy of the grapevine: strong, tenacious, pushing forward.

MEANING:

VINE reflects the idea of displaying over-control during inner weakness. When there is a lot of willpower, but the body cannot cope because it is overloaded.

## STAGE OF DEVELOPMENT

2-4 years: stage of the will, when the child has to exert willpower to be heard.

CORE OF THE TRAUMA

"If I loosen my grip — everything will fall apart."

"I cannot relax — I need to press.

"If I do not hold everything, the world will become chaos."

## EFFECT

The remedy relieves internal rigidity,

reduces the need for control,

gives soft confidence, empathy, and calm, mature strength.

IDEA OF THE REMEDY

True strength does not require pressure.
It naturally manifests when you believe in yourself and trust the world.

MESSAGE OF THE REMEDY

- strength should not hurt
- softness is a form of mature strength
- trust is more important than control
- connection is more important than domination
- the world is not held together by your tension.

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

- A grapevine stops choking everything around, sprouts soft leaves, grows upwards to the sun freely, without a tight grip.

## RITUAL

- take 2-5 drops of VINE under the tongue
- imagine a rigid rod at the solar plexus
- place your hand on it, imagine how, with an exhale, the rigid rod melts and becomes light.
- imagine how the light spreads through the chest, shoulders, head.
- say the phrases:

* my strength is calm and clear
* I trust myself and my work
* I am calm in any

circumstances

* I am confident and inspire others

* I can relax.
