BACH HORNBEAM REMEDY

ESSENCE

This is the energy of hornbeam.

A light, lively tree with very dense and strong wood.

On the outside — softness and flexibility.

Inside — composure and strength.

MEANING

This is the energy of engaging in life.

It is the energy that helps

start, enter the flow, get involved in action.

This remedy removes heaviness before starting,
restores clarity, vigor, and inner readiness.

HORNBEAM is suitable

When a person:

• feels tired before beginning
• procrastinates
• cannot get going
• feels "I can’t manage"
• experiences boredom and lack of interest

But once they start — energy appears.

The remedy provides a gentle launch and engagement.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

5–8 years — the stage of activity and interest.

This is the period when a person:

• learns to engage in activity
• develops attention
• forms interest in the world

If pressure or boredom arises during this period,
a state forms:

“I don't want to start”

CORE OF THE TRAUMA

“I am tired even before starting.”

“It’s hard for me to get into it.”

“I have no strength to start.”

## EFFECT

The remedy gently:

• removes mental heaviness

• restores clarity

• activates energy

• helps to start

A feeling appears:

“I can simply start”

MESSAGE OF THE REMEDY

• energy comes in action

• the first step triggers the flow

• movement generates strength

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

Morning forest.

First the fog, stillness, sleep.

Nothing is desired.

But the first ray of sun appears.

The leaves begin to move.

The forest wakes up.

And with this, the movement of life appears.

## RITUAL

• take 2–5 drops of HORNBEAM under the tongue

• place your hand on the forehead

• imagine fog in your head

• with each breath, a ray of light passes through your head

• the fog clears

• clarity and lightness appear

Say the phrases:

• I start easily

• energy comes in movement

• I am engaged in life

It's easy for me to act

⸻

Experience shows that the remedy gives a sense of engagement and clarity.

The heaviness and procrastination disappear, and a feeling appears:

"I just start — and the energy comes."
