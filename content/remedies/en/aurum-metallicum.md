Studied working with gold (the Aurum remedy in homeopathy).

In psychotherapy, key archetypes are distinguished: father and mother.

In homeopathy, the Aurum remedy (gold) is used to work through relationships with the father.

This remedy is responsible for dignity, confidence, personal value. It increases calmness and strength.

But it was also interesting to once again realize that it is premature to start homeotherapy with gold.

This remedy will not work to its full extent until previous levels have been completed.

As alchemists believed,

Gold is the pinnacle of matter.

We begin working with the vibrations of gold at the middle or higher stage of homeotherapy.

Before that, we still need to go through the vibrations of simple metals.

By integrating the vibrations of iron, silver, mercury, and other substances into the energy structure, we strengthen the power of our personality, gradually bringing ourselves closer to the ideal.

This is how work in metaphysical homeopathy proceeds.
