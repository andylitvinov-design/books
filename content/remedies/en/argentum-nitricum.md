ARGENTUM NITRICUM

(Silver nitrate).

This is a valuable preparation that we use in the middle stages of therapy.

It is relevant when you already have a certain confidence in yourself, but become lost in the face of special, responsible events.

The preparation helps to maintain confidence and composure in the most difficult situations.

## RATIONALE

1) Silver nitrate is a compound of silver with explosive nitric acid. Physically, it causes trembling, irritation (symptoms of acute stress).

2) Archetypically, the preparation reflects the mind standing on the verge of overheating. This is the archetype of the Magician standing on the edge of chaos.

The preparation teaches: to slow down, to trust, to listen to intuition, and not only fear.

This provides stability through inner support and acceptance.

## INDICATIONS

- panic attacks, fear of losing control, fuss, hurry

- fear of public speaking, making a mistake, being embarrassed

- trembling, diarrhea, feeling that everything

will collapse if I can't cope.

- fidgetiness, haste, inability to slow down.

## IMAGES / METAPHORS

- A person running along the edge of a cliff, not knowing why.

- A person standing on the edge of a bridge, obsessed with the desire to take a step, but paralyzed by fear. He flits between determination and panic.

- A mirror reflecting fears until the person sees their true face.

- A crystal born from chaos.

## ARCHETYPE

- Alchemist on the edge of chaos

- Frightened dreamer

- Panicker at the start

5. DEVELOPMENT STAGE (according to Biodynamics)

5–8 years: Structure of opinions — a period in which self-confidence is formed, the ability to express opinions, not to be afraid of mistakes and judgment. The remedy helps to overcome fear of criticism and gain inner support.

## EFFECT

- Calms the mind, reduces mental anxiety;

- Helps stop the flow of obsessive thoughts;

- Restores trust in oneself and in life;

- Balances emotions

and the body;

- Strengthens willpower and clarity.

## ARCHETYPE IDEA

You are not obligated to know and control everything.

You can go step by step, trusting yourself.

Your strength is in clarity and freedom from fear.

## LESSON

- Making mistakes is safe.

- Control does not always lead to success.

- True confidence is born not in knowing all the answers, but in trusting yourself and the path.

## TRANSFORMATION

From an anxious fugitive — to a conscious mage.

From the fear of mistakes — to acceptance of growth.

From haste — to wise movement.

10. AFFIRMATIONS:

- I move forward with trust.

- I do not need to know everything in advance.

- I am calm inside.

- My wisdom is in clarity, not in haste.

- I allow myself to go slowly and precisely.

11. Archetypal resource:

- The ability to react quickly, finding solutions in stressful situations.

- The power of intellect, intuition, strategic thinking.

- A powerful creative imagination that, purified from fear,

becomes genius.

## RITUAL

- prepare a candle, a mirror, a bowl of water

- look into the mirror, see your eyes, say, I am

- put lightning-chaos on paper (draw spirals or write thoughts)

- Light the candle, pour water into the bowl, and look at how the candle is reflected in the water. This is your bridge to the light. Say: "I enter the light, my impulses serve my soul."

SUMMARY.

Argentum Nitricum is a remedy for the mind exhausted by anxiety and for the soul striving for freedom but afraid to break free. It helps to get out of panic, slow down, ground yourself, open your imagination, and act with love, not fear.

Key idea of the archetype:

"Your speed is not in running away, but in clarity. Your strength is not in anticipating catastrophes, but in trusting the step."
