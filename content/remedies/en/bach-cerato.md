BACH CERATO Remedy

ESSENCE

This is the energy of cerato — a plant with delicate, upward-reaching flowers.

It reaches for the light, but simultaneously seems not to fully hold its axis.

MEANING

This is the state when a person does not trust themselves.

CERATO is suitable

When a person:

• constantly asks for advice
• doubts their decisions
• seeks confirmation from others
• changes their opinion under the influence of others
• does not trust their intuition

They may know the answer…

but still ask.

The remedy restores inner support within oneself.

This vibration helps

to stop seeking answers externally and start hearing oneself.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

5–8 years — the stage of forming one's own opinion.

This is the period when a person:

• learns to think independently
• forms their own point of view
• begins to express themselves

If there was no support during this period,
a state develops:
"it is better for me to trust others rather than myself"

CORE

TRAUMAS

"I might be wrong."

"Others know better."

"I am not confident in myself."

## EFFECT

The remedy gently:

• enhances contact with inner knowledge

• reduces dependence on others' opinions

• strengthens confidence

• helps in decision making

A feeling arises:

"I know what is right for me"

MESSAGE OF THE REMEDY

• the answer is already within

• you can trust yourself

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

A person stands at a crossroads.

At first, they look around,

listen to others, doubt.

The noise of voices around.

But gradually everything quiets down.

Only the quiet inner voice remains.

And they take a step —

no longer doubting.

## RITUAL

• take 2–5 drops of CERATO under the tongue

• place a hand on the forehead

• imagine numerous voices around

• with each exhale, they fade away

• only one remains — the inner voice

• feel its clarity

Say the phrases:

• I trust myself

• I hear my truth

• my decisions are correct

for me

• I rely on myself

Experience shows that the remedy restores the feeling of inner peace, stability, clarity.

Doubt and dependence on others' opinions disappear, and a state appears:

"I know — and that is enough."
