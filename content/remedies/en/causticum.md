CAUSTICUM

Another deep remedy that restores the sense of truth and dignity, even if you are not appreciated and it seems that the whole world is against you.

The remedy helps to withstand an aggressive environment and strengthen the Power of Spirit, Power of Personality.

## BASE

Slaked lime combined with sulfuric acid.

It is a caustic, burning substance.

In homeopathy, it helps to withstand caustic words and remarks in order to regain your strength, voice, and sense of justice.

## ARCHETYPE

- Prometheus, chained, fighting for his truth, love for people.

- A blazing warrior, but immobilized and petrified.

- Fiery reformer.

## IMAGE

- A candle illuminating the world, even while burning out

- A frozen river, under which a hot river of protest boils

- A person with a stone face but a fiery heart, fighting for an idea, but shedding tears and losing strength.

## INDICATIONS

- Feeling of being a victim and injustice

- Loss of faith, apathy, numbness

-

psycho-emotional exhaustion

- stiffness, dryness, loss of voice

CAUSTICUM IS RELEVANT

If there is an unconscious demand on yourself to be a hero who has no right to vulnerability.

"I must be correct, strong, fair, otherwise I betray myself."

CORRECTION:

"I am valuable not only when I am strong.

My vitality is valuable even in moments of weakness."

## EFFECT

- removes the shackles of stiffness, numbness

- gives strength and courage to speak, to express yourself.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

- 5-8 years Opinion Structure:

- My voice is important, I must express myself, but I am afraid.

## LESSON

- You can allow your feelings to exist. Experiencing, feeling pain is normal.

Tears are not a weakness, but a restoration of the flow of life.

- When you let go of control, feelings, tension, your breath returns. You gain the strength of flexibility.

- You do not need to try to be perfect, a rock, to earn respect.

You can be

myself.

- You should not sacrifice yourself for the world. You are valuable alive. Take care of yourself.

CONFLICT

- Serving others while ignoring yourself.

- Anger at injustice, but prohibition on feelings, weakness, and rage.

## ARCHETYPE IDEA

- You don't have to be a stone to be strong. Allow yourself to tremble, cry, speak — and you will reclaim your fire.

AFFIRMATIONS

- I can feel.

- I can be gentle and strong.

- My voice matters.

- Justice begins with my word.

## RITUAL

- Sit in front of a mirror, light a candle (symbol of inner fire).

Feel the stiffness of feelings, like Prometheus.

- Look into your eyes. Say: "I am here. I am alive. My word matters. My pain is light seeking its way."

- Experience the feelings through your body (pain, anger, etc.)

- Place your hand on your throat, breathe — imagine the flame inside softly warming the stiffness.

- Mentally cast off the chains. Whisper, then say loudly:

"I have the right

to speak. I am freeing myself.
