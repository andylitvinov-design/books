###message169

PHOSPHORICUM ACIDUM

A valuable remedy that I prescribe in the initial stages of working with trauma.

It helps when there is no more strength, when the soul and body are exhausted. When there is apathy and devastation after stress and loss.

The remedy restores strength and hope.

INDICATIONS:

- chronic fatigue, apathy

- emotional burnout

- loss of motivation, concentration

- exhaustion, weakened immunity.

EFFECT

The remedy restores balance of strength and interest in life.

LOOK:

- A withered tree in the desert that was once green and strong, but has lost its sap due to a long drought.

- A man sitting detachedly on a desert plain under a gray sky, but in the distance a light can be seen - a symbol of hope and rebirth.

BASIS

Phosphoric acid - slightly destroys metals, absorbs moisture well.

Symbolizes the dissolution of the old for the birth of the new.

Participates in energy metabolism in the body.

Gently removes leaks and restores energy balance.

ARCHETYPE

- Tired traveler

- Devastated

wanderer

- Burnt soul.

ARCHETYPE IDEA:

- loss (of resources, meanings)

and restoration through connection with the deep sources of vitality.

SHADOW OF THE ARCHETYPE

- reluctance to admit fatigue, an attempt to “hold on” despite exhaustion.

ARCHETYPE RESOURCE

- increased sensitivity to beauty and intuition,

- the opportunity to recover and replenish through connection with yourself and sources of strength,

- finding new meanings after the crisis.

STRESS/LESSON

- loss of energy when wanting to live actively

- accept your weaknesses and limitations

- take care of yourself

TRANSFORMATION

(Meanings, objectives):

1. Realize that a crisis is not an end, but an opportunity for a new beginning.

2. Restore energy and self-confidence, inner light through accepting past experiences.

3. Finding new sources of energy and inspiration.

ALCHEMY

- Nigredo (black stage of alchemy): the state of decline that precedes rebirth.

- Phosphorus symbolizes “fire”, which is exhausted but can be revived.

PHOSPHORICUM ACIDUM

The remedy gently restores strength, restores joy and passion for life.

It helps a person overcome deep apathy, see new perspectives and feel alive again.
