PHOSPHORIC ACID

A valuable remedy that I prescribe in the early stages of dealing with trauma.

It helps when there is no strength left, when the soul and body are exhausted. When there is apathy and emptiness after stress and loss.

The remedy restores strength and hope.

## INDICATIONS

- chronic fatigue, apathy

- emotional burnout

- loss of motivation, concentration

- exhaustion, weakened immunity.

## EFFECT

The remedy restores balance of strength and interest in life.

## IMAGE

- A dried-up tree in the desert that was once green and strong but lost its sap due to prolonged drought.

- A person sitting detachedly on a deserted plain under a gray sky, but light is visible in the distance – a symbol of hope and rebirth.

## BASIS

Phosphoric acid – slightly corrodes metals, absorbs moisture well.

It symbolizes the dissolution of the old for the birth of the new.

In the body, it participates in energy metabolism.

Gently removes leaks, restores energy balance.

## ARCHETYPE

- Tired traveler

- Exhausted wanderer

- Burned-out soul.

## ARCHETYPE IDEA

- loss (of resources, meanings)

and restoration through connection with deep sources of life force.

## ARCHETYPE SHADOW

- unwillingness to acknowledge fatigue, trying to "hold on" despite exhaustion.

## ARCHETYPE RESOURCE

- heightened sensitivity to beauty and intuition,

- the ability to recover and fill oneself through connection with oneself and sources of strength,

- finding new meanings after a crisis.

STRESS / LESSON

- loss of energy when wishing to live actively

- accept your weaknesses and limitations

- take care of yourself

## TRANSFORMATION

(Meanings, tasks):

1. Realize that a crisis is not the end but an opportunity for a new beginning.

2. Restore energy and faith in oneself, inner light through acceptance of past experience.

3. Finding new sources

energy and inspiration.

## ALCHEMY

- Nigredo (the black stage of alchemy): a state of decline that precedes rebirth.

- Phosphorus symbolizes the 'fire' that is exhausted but can be revived.

PHOSPHORICUM ACIDUM

The remedy gently restores strength, brings back joy and enthusiasm for life.

It helps a person overcome deep apathy, see new perspectives, and feel alive again.
