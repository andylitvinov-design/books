MEZEREUM

(Wolf's-bane, shrub).

Preparation of the burnt heart.

Have you ever felt that there is a scream under your skin? That the pain is so deep that you are even ashamed to show it?

Mezereum helps with frozen, unprocessed pain, especially after rejection, humiliation, and violence.

It pulls you out of shock, resentment, and isolation. It helps to feel your strength and dignity.

## BASE

A poisonous shrub whose sap causes burns, irritations, ulcers.

Symbolically: it burns upon touch.

Like a person who fears intimacy but suffers from loneliness.

ARCHETYPES

- Burnt child (trusted and was hurt)

- Ice prince/princess (beautiful but distant from fear of pain)

- Ash guardian (under a mask of calm, afraid to return to feelings to avoid getting burned)

- Glass person (can crack from any word)

- Alchemist of pain (accepted and healed the pain)

## IMAGE

- Burnt child behind glass:

He wants to be hugged, but even the slightest touch causes pain. He is silent, watching, but afraid to step forward.

- A child in an icy room, reaching hands toward the flame.

## INDICATIONS

- early trauma of touch, violence, rejection

- fear of physical contact, intimacy

- deep shame about the body and feelings

- Thoughts: "I am dirty," "there is something wrong with me"

- Frozen emotions: anger, resentment, sadness

- Isolation, a shell around the soul

- Physically: burns, skin rashes, itching, and pain upon contact

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

- Age: 0.1–1.5 years (stage of physical contact, intimacy)

- Conflict: Desire for love / Fear of pain

- Trauma: "I was touched when I was too vulnerable — and it hurt"

- Mask: I don't need contact. I am self-sufficient.

## IDEA

To come alive — you need to start feeling again.

MESSAGE AND LESSON

Yes, you were burned. But you are still here.

Your pain is not you.

You can again

to feel — and not burn out.

You have the right to step out of loneliness.

You have the right to be touched — with love.

AFFIRMATIONS

- I allow myself to feel.

- Touch can be kind.

- My body is not an enemy. It wants love.

- I restore boundaries and let warmth into the places where there was pain.

## TRANSFORMATION

- Before: The soul is frozen; any feelings burn.

- After: Contact is possible.

The soul warms up.

Touch is support, not a threat.

The body is warm, alive.

RITUAL (alchemy triad)

- Meeting with pain.

Place your hand on your heart or a painful area. Say:

"I know you were in pain. I am with you. I do not reject you."

- Cleansing.

Apply (or imagine mentally) a soft warm cream, water, or oil to the body. Say: "You can feel — and it is safe."

- Returning to warmth

Close your eyes, hug yourself. Imagine that you are a baby in loving hands. Say: "I am home. I am loved. I can feel."

I can live.

## ALCHEMY

- Nigredo. The personality is scorched, covered with crusts, but inside there is still a flame of desire to be alive and loved.

- Mezereum = fire trapped under ash, waiting for healing to become light, not pain.
