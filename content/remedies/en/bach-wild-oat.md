BACH WILD OAT Remedy

ESSENCE

This is the energy of wild oat.

It grows freely, without rules, without strict lines.

MEANING

This is the energy that allows you to take the first step, to start acting.

This remedy adds courage, speed, decisiveness.

It accelerates moving forward and at the same time gives energy, inspiration, and strength for your undertakings.

WILD OAT is suitable

When a person:

• does not understand what they want to do

• is searching for meaning and purpose

• is not satisfied with current life, feels capable of more, but does not know where to apply themselves.

The remedy gives a vector and impulse forward.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

12–25 years — the stage of identity and choice of path.

This is a period when a person:

• is searching for their place in life

• forms meaning and direction.

If during this period there is no clarity or support,

a state may form:

"I don't know where to go."

CORE TRAUMA

"I can do a lot, but I don't know

where to go."

"I don't feel my place."

"I'm afraid of making the wrong choice."

## EFFECT

The remedy gently:

• clarifies inner desires

• enhances contact with oneself

• helps to feel your direction

• gives an impulse forward

A feeling appears:

"I am walking my own path."

MESSAGE OF THE REMEDY

• you already know your path

• the choice comes from within

• meaning unfolds in movement.

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

A field of wild oats.

At first, the ears grow chaotically,

leaning in different directions,

moving in the wind without direction.

But gradually a single stream of wind appears.

And all the ears begin to move in one direction.

The field becomes alive,

but already gathered and directed.

## RITUAL

- take 2–5 drops of WILD OAT under the tongue

- place your hand on the solar plexus area

- imagine many roads diverging in different directions.

With each exhale, the extra roads dissolve.

One remains —

clear and radiant.

- Imagine yourself starting to walk along it.

Say the phrases:

• I feel my path

• my life has direction

• I choose from inner clarity

• my energy moves in the right direction

• I realize my potential.

Experience shows that the medication gives a sense of an inner vector and purpose.

Restlessness and uncertainty disappear, a feeling arises:

'I know my path.'
