KALIUM PHOSPHORICUM

This remedy has proven itself excellently in situations when everything seems lost, when you want to hide and not interact with the world.

The remedy gives a sense of personal strength, pride, and self-confidence.

The remedy helps in a state of nervous exhaustion.

For comparison,

- Carbo works with physical exhaustion;

- Phosphoricum Acidum is effective for emotional exhaustion, apathy;

- Kalium Phosphoricum is effective for mental exhaustion, anxiety, detachment.

The remedy quickly restores your strength, confidence, and interest in life.

BASIS: potassium phosphate.

Used to support the nervous system, activate electrolytes, and potassium.

It stabilizes solutions and is used for plant growth.

INDICATIONS / SHADOW:

- stress, exhaustion, difficulty concentrating, powerlessness, helplessness

- fear of not being strong enough to overcome obstacles.

## EFFECT

- the remedy

Acts as a tonic, restoring strength and courage.

## ARCHETYPE

- The exhausted thinker.

- The resurrecting light.

## IMAGE

- A torch that is lit at night, illuminating the path.

- A person walking through a misty valley, yet seeing a bright path ahead.

FEATURE OF THE PREPARATION

- That it adds confidence, courage to express oneself, and pursue one's goals.

BODYNAMICS:

- Opinion stage 5-9 years.

Removes the fear of expressing oneself, stating one’s opinion, and defending one’s interests.

## ALCHEMY

- Third stage (yellow):

Awakening the inner light.

At the yellow stage, a person begins to see their place in the world, realizing their strengths and goals.

LESSON / TRANSFORMATION

- "There is light even in a dark valley." Even in the most difficult situations, there is an inner source of strength.

- Free oneself from overfatigue, from the fear of being imperfect.

- Accept yourself as you are, find your light and pride in it.
