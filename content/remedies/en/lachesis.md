LACHESIS (snake venom) remedy

A remedy for bright, fiery women who are often expressive and sometimes ready to burn everything around them with their emotions.

It helps in situations of

- jealousy

- fear of being oppressed and restricted.

It is relevant for

- the need for freedom and self-expression

- feelings of jealousy and rivalry

- expressiveness and emotionality.

## ARCHETYPE

- temptress

- jealous manipulator

- freedom-loving rebel

I observe how this remedy affects my patients.

The EFFECT is interesting.

When using the remedy, the volcano of emotions decreases.

Instead of a storm inside and outside, a sense of clarity and a light interest in others and the world arises.

As if the storm passes and dawn comes.

If you apply Lachesis, a miracle happens. Anger is replaced by mercy. It becomes more interesting not to rebel, but to listen.

Inner calm and depth are strengthened;

Silence.

The world around becomes clearer and more pure.
