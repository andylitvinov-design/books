STAR OF BETHLEHEM Remedy

Experience shows that the remedy provides a deep sense of comfort.

Internal freezing and pain go away, and a feeling appears:

"I am alive again and I feel."

ESSENCE

The remedy gently gathers and harmonizes the internal state.

It is the energy of comfort, restoration, and healing after shock.

It is the energy of the Star of Bethlehem.

A delicate white flower, resembling a glowing star.

MEANING

It is the energy that restores wholeness after trauma.

It is the energy that helps to

gently experience pain and gather oneself again.

This remedy works where there has been:

• shock

• trauma

• severe emotional distress

STAR OF BETHLEHEM is suitable

When a person:

• has experienced a significant event

• feels frozen or empty

• cannot let go of the past

• carries pain within themselves

• feels internal fracture

The remedy provides gentle restoration and comfort.

## STAGE OF DEVELOPMENT

Any age —

A timeless remedy.

It works with:

• acute injuries

• childhood experiences

• deep shocks

This is the level of:

restoring the integrity of the psyche

CORE OF TRAUMA

"It was too painful."

"I couldn’t go through it."

"I feel like I broke inside."

## EFFECT

The remedy gently:

• removes the effects of shock

• restores sensitivity

• helps to process the trauma

• restores inner integrity

A feeling appears:

"I am whole again"

MESSAGE OF THE REMEDY

• you can heal

• the pain can be endured

• you are not broken

• there is wholeness inside

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

A dark night.

Inside: cold, silence, and emptiness.

As if everything has stopped.

And suddenly a soft light appears —

a small star.

This light begins to expand.

It warms the space,

and gradually life returns.

## RITUAL

• take 2–5 drops of STAR OF BETHLEHEM under the tongue

• place your hand on your heart

• imagine

dark space inside

• a small star appears in the center

• with each breath it becomes brighter

• light fills the chest, the body, the whole space

• warmth and softness appear

Say the phrases:

• I am healing

• I accept my pain

• I am becoming whole again

• there is light within me
