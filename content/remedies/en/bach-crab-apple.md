BACH CRAB APPLE REMEDY

Has a gentle, cleansing, and very harmonizing effect.

Suitable for people who experience feelings of inner impurity, shame, or embarrassment, and feel that "something is wrong with me."

Experience shows that the remedy provides a sense of inner cleanliness and self-acceptance.

The obsessive feeling of imperfection disappears, and a feeling arises:

"I am fine."

ESSENCE OF THE REMEDY

CRAB APPLE — wild apple tree.

This is a tree with small, often uneven fruits.

From the outside, they may seem imperfect, but inside they remain alive and clean.

In Bach's system, this remedy is called the "system cleanser."

MEANING

CRAB APPLE reflects the theme of inner cleanliness and self-acceptance.

It is the state when a person:

feels shy about themselves

feels inner "impurity"

focuses too much on minor flaws

worries about appearance or body

feels embarrassment and a desire to "cleanse."

Sometimes this manifests as:

perfectionism

disgust

overly strong desire for cleanliness

rejection of one's own body.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

0–1.5 years — stage of contact and acceptance.

This is the period when the basic feeling is formed:

"everything is fine with me, I am accepted."

If during this period there arises an experience of:

shame

rejection

criticism of the body

it may solidify a deep sense:

"there is something wrong with me."

CORE OF THE TRAUMA

"I am not clean enough."

"There is something wrong with me."

"I could be rejected if they see the real me."

"I need to fix myself."

Inside accumulates:

shame

embarrassment

self-criticism

feeling of defect.

## EFFECT

The medication gently:

removes the feeling of inner shame

restores the sense of cleanliness

reduces fixation on flaws

restores contact with the body

returns natural self-acceptance.

A feeling appears:

"I am natural and alive."

IDEA

OF THE REMEDY

Perfection is not in flawlessness,
but in living naturalness.

When a person accepts themselves,
their energy begins to flow freely.

MESSAGE OF THE REMEDY

you are already good enough
the body is part of your strength
purity is an inner state
naturalness is more important than perfection
life loves the living, not the perfect.

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

In the forest stands a wild apple tree.
Its fruits are small, imperfect,
but they are full of life.
The wind passes through the branches,
the petals fall to the ground and turn into soil.
From this soil, new flowers are born.
Life accepts everything and turns it into growth.

## RITUAL

take 2–5 drops of CRAB APPLE under the tongue
place your hand on the abdomen
imagine in your body the feeling of impurity or shame.
With each breath, a pure flow of water passes through the body.
It gently washes away tension,
and the body begins to glow with pure light.
Imagine an apple blossom opening inside you.

Say the phrases:

I accept myself

My body is clean and alive

I am worthy of love and acceptance

I allow myself to be natural

I am okay.
