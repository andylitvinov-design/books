🧪 OOPHORINUM

A hormonal homeopathic preparation suitable for women.

It gives a sense of special inner fullness, harmony, and abundance. Everything is well, everything exists, everything is calm. This is close to the image of the 3rd Tarot Arcana.

🌙 Archetype

The archetype of the feminine Moon, the rhythm, and the secret breath of the body. This is a preparation for cycles, transitions, and hormonal waves. Its theme is female identity, sexuality, fertility, and a sense of self-worth in connection with the female body.

It is relevant when a woman has lost contact with her natural rhythm: after childbirth, abortions, hormonal imbalances, during menopause, exhaustion, or excessive shame about femininity.

This is a preparation for initiation into the inner Priestess: acceptance of the body, feminine nature, and its mysteries.

🌿 Basis

Oophorinum is made from the ovaries of animals.

Symbolically: a source of female hormones; a connection with the cycle of life, blood, love, sexuality, and motherhood.

Used for:

- infertility,

- menstrual cycle disorders,

- depression,

- hot flashes and hormonal crises.

💥 Indications

- Menstrual disorders, menopause, infertility.

- Deep fatigue after losses, abortions, childbirth.

- Fear of femininity, shame about the body.

- Depression against the background of hormonal crises.

- Sexual frigidity or, on the contrary, hypersensitivity.

- Mood swings, insomnia.

- Weight and metabolism disorders.

- Inner emptiness, feeling "I am not a real woman".

🔥 Archetypes

- Moon Priestess who keeps the secret of cycles.

- Woman tired of motherhood, having lost herself.

- Offended Venus, ashamed of her body.

- Woman in transition — in menopause, in shifts, seeking support.

- Moon that returns a woman's radiance.

🖼 Images

- Moon over a dark lake.

- Woman with a closed face, embracing her belly.

- Silvery water that restores strength.

A woman standing on the border of night and dawn.

🌸 Effect of the preparation

- Restores the harmony of hormonal rhythm.

- Relieves shame, anxiety, inner discord.

- Provides gentle acceptance of femininity.

- Helps reconnect with sexuality without guilt.

- Relieves fatigue and emotional exhaustion.

- Supports the body during transitions (puberty, childbirth, menopause).

🧱 Development stages

0–1.5 years: contact with the mother's body, feeling of warmth

3–6 years: acceptance of love and sexuality.

12–18 years: formation of identity and femininity.

Menopause: transition, search for new value and wholeness.

💡 Idea of the preparation

Femininity is not only fertility.

You are a source of life, love, light, and softness.

Your rhythms are strength, not weakness.

✨ Message / Lesson

You have the right to be a woman — in any form. Your body is a temple.

You can receive and give love without shame.

You are beautiful in transitions, because

what exactly makes you alive.

🌺 Affirmations

I embrace my femininity.

My body is wise and sacred.

I can be myself at any age.

I am worthy of love and care.

I am connected to the Moon, and its rhythms are within me.

🔄 Transformation

Before: shame, hormonal swings, fatigue, feeling inadequate.

After: harmony, body acceptance, wholeness, radiance.

🖌 Visualization / Meditation

Imagine yourself by a lake on a moonlit night. The water reflects the moon.

You touch the water with your palms and wash your face.

Your body breathes in rhythm with the waves.

You whisper: "I am a woman. I am alive. I shine."

🔮 Ritual

Light a candle. Hug yourself, place your hand on your belly.

Say: "I embrace my rhythms. My body is my temple." Drink warm herbal tea (sage, lemon balm) and feel the warmth entering your belly.
