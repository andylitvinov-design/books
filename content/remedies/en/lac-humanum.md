LAC HUMANUM

A preparation created on the basis of breast milk.

A very special preparation.

The preparation is relevant in situations when there is a gaping hole of non-belonging inside a person.

Contact with a maternal figure in early childhood was absent or interrupted. Or there was not enough acceptance and support.

When there is emptiness, abandonment, loss of strength inside.

The preparation quickly brings one from a feeling of unworthiness and desire to hide to a readiness to open up to loving contact.

RELEVANCE

The preparation is needed in cases:

- when you feel like a 'stranger among people,' you are not seen,
- longing inside for lost warmth, belonging,
- feeling of alienation, 'I am not from here,'
- dissolving into others,
- fear of rejection, desire to be good,
- powerlessness, feeling of isolation among people,
- insecurity about the right to exist,
- allergies as a rejection of the world.

## BASIS

- breast milk, which gives the feeling: 'I am'

love. I exist.

## ARCHETYPE

- Infant who lost the Mother of the World

- Light longing for the Source

- Soul searching for Home

- Rejected soul (does not feel unconditional acceptance)

## IMAGE

- newborn in an incubator behind glass,

- child who hears everything but does not feel warmth.

## EFFECT

- Dissolving longing and distancing.

- Feeling: I can be loved.

- Restoring connection with oneself and others.

STAGE:

0-1 year

## IDEA

You are not a mistake.

You were born, which means you have the right to exist.

You are part of humanity.

And even if you didn’t receive love then — you can nourish yourself with it now.
