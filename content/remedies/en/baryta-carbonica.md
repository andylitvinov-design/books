BARYTA CARBONICA

The remedy works with feelings of powerlessness and helplessness.

It

gives support when it feels like everything is falling apart and you want to hide. It restores composure in stress.

INTRODUCTION

Have you ever felt that the world is too big, and you are too small?

That you are not ready for life yet.

And it is better to stay in the shadow, not to stick out?

Baryta carbonica is the fear of growing up. A remedy for the forgotten, the rejected, those who are afraid to be adults.

Those who think: "I am not like everyone else… I cannot handle it…"

## FOUNDATION

Barium carbonate is a heavy, dense substance. Symbolizes heaviness, constraint, suppression.

In homeopathy - it restores strength trapped in the grip of fear.

ARCHETYPES:

- Frozen child (wants love but is ashamed of themselves)

- Small child in the corner (afraid to speak)

- Wounded child who is afraid to become a hero.

IMAGES:

- Small child in an enormous school uniform.

- Forgotten teddy bear in the corner of the wall with children's drawings.

- Empty stage, on which a silhouette is afraid

start talking.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

- Age: 5–8 years

- Structure: Opinions, evaluation

- Mask: Quiet, inconspicuous, detached.

- Conflict: "I can't cope," "I'm not ready," "I will be judged."

At the core lies a trauma of rejection, devaluation, shame for one's weakness, and fear of being unrecognized again.

## INDICATIONS

- Shyness, fear of showing oneself

- Shame for not fitting in

- Low self-esteem

- Expectation of judgment, fear of authority, avoidance of responsibility

- Desire to hide, not be noticed

- Insecurity in body, actions, words

- Avoidance of contact, isolation

- Feeling: I am incapable, unworthy.

- Physically: weakness, lethargy.

## EFFECT

- Release from inner numbness

- Thawing of tension in the body

- Letting go of the shame of expressing oneself and anxiety that "I am not like others."

- Acceptance of the weakness of one's inner child

- More courage to try, to express oneself,

asking for support

- Gradual acquisition of inner strength and pride in oneself.

You don't have to be perfect to be valuable. Growth begins with allowing yourself to be yourself.

You have the right to make mistakes.

You have the right to speak, express yourself, try, explore.

Don't be afraid to look foolish.

As long as you live — you are learning.
