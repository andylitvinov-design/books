### message481

BARYTA CARBONICA

The remedy works against feelings of powerlessness and helplessness.

He gives support when everything seems to be falling apart and you want to hide. It brings back endurance under stress.

INTRODUCTION

Have you ever felt that the world is too big and you are too small?

That you are not ready for life yet.

And is it better to stay in the shadows and keep a low profile?

Baryta carbonica is the fear of growing up. The remedy of the forgotten, the rejected, those who are afraid of being an adult.

Who thinks: “I’m not like everyone else... I can’t cope...”

BASE:

Barium carbonate is a heavy, dense substance. Symbolizes heaviness, tightness, restraint.

In homeopathy, it returns strength that is shackled in the grip of fear.

ARCHETYPES:

- Frozen child (wants love, but is ashamed of himself)

- Small child in the corner (afraid to speak)

- A wounded child who is afraid of becoming a hero.

LOOKS:

- A small child in

huge school uniform.

- A forgotten bear in the corner of the wall with children's drawings.

- An empty stage on which the silhouette is afraid to start talking.

DEVELOPMENT STAGE

- Age: 5–8 years

- Structure: Opinions, evaluation

- Mask: Quiet, inconspicuous, distant.

- Conflict: “I can’t cope,” “I’m not mature enough,” “They’ll judge me.”

It is based on the trauma of rejection, devaluation, shame for one’s weakness and fear of being unrecognized again.

INDICATIONS:

- Shyness, fear of showing up

- Shame for inadequacy

- Low self-esteem

- Expectation of condemnation, fear of authority, avoidance of responsibility

- The desire to hide, not to be noticed

- Uncertainty in the body, in actions, in words

- Avoidance of contact, isolation

- Feeling: I am not capable, I am not worthy.

- Physically: weakness, lethargy.

EFFECT

- Liberation from internal numbness

- Unfreezing tension in the body

- Letting go of the shame of expressing yourself and the anxiety that “I’m not like that.”

- Accepting the weakness of your inner child

- More courage to try,

show up, ask for support

- Gradual acquisition of inner core and pride in oneself.

IDEA

You don't have to be perfect to be valuable. Growth begins with permission to be yourself.

MESSAGE AND LESSON

You have the right to make mistakes.

TV you have the right to speak, show up, try, search.

Don't be afraid to seem stupid.

While you are alive, you are learning.

AFFIRMATIONS

- I allow myself to grow - at my own pace.

- I don't have to be perfect to be worthy.

- I allow myself to learn, make mistakes, grow up.

- I'm important. Even if I'm afraid.

TRANSFORMATION

- Before: Shame about yourself, frozen feeling of “I’m not enough,” avoidance of life.

- After: Accepting yourself with your inner child, courage to be, growth from within.

RITUAL:

Draw yourself small and pet him. And then draw light behind it and a step forward.

KEY

I have the right to grow.

My childishness is not a shame

and the root of my future tree.
