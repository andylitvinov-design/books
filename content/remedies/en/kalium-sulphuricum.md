KALIUM SULPHURICUM

This is a remedy that helps to get out of the "swamp" of life, old unnecessary ties, and limitations.

The remedy is suitable in situations when you feel restricted, isolated, lacking warmth and love, and have no strength to change anything in your life.

EXAMPLE:

- When this remedy is prescribed, inner strength and the potential to take action are activated.

## INDICATIONS

- worries, being stuck in situations or problems;

- inability to let go of the old to encounter the new;

- emotional isolation, melancholy, loss of spirit;

- heaviness, fatigue, need for cleansing.

BASE: Potassium sulfate:

- stimulates metabolic processes in the body,
- promotes cleansing and restoration of balance,
- stimulates growth and resilience in plants.

## EFFECT

- stimulates the movement of energy, cleansing the body and soul.

## IMAGE

- An old tree covered with moss, which needs renewal.

to start growing again;

- Waiting for rain that will wash away the dust and refresh the earth.

## ARCHETYPE

- Cleansing Spirit

- Liberator

- Detoxifier, Regenerator

- The one who comes out of the shadows into the light.

IDEA / CONFLICT:

- Desire for renewal and fear of change

- Longing for warmth and light, but fear of leaving the shadow.

## SHADOW OF THE ARCHETYPE

- Getting stuck, guilt, fear, tendency to isolate oneself, despite the desire for warmth.

## RESOURCE

- Ability to complete processes, cleanse, and move forward.

- There is potential for ease and renewal.

## LESSON

- Realize that the completion of one stage is the beginning of a new one.

- Accept changes as a way of renewal.

- Step out of the shadow into the light.

MEANING:

- Warmth and light —

are the result of cleansing.

SPIRITUAL TASK:

- Completion of an old cycle, transition to a new stage of life.

KALIUM SULPHURICUM

- is a symbol of renewal that helps a person let go of the past, regain lightness and

warmth, move forward, cleansing body and soul. He teaches to get rid of the unnecessary, to find light even in the darkest corners, and to find strength for new growth.
