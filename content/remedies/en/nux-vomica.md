NUX VOMICA

An interesting remedy. It is often prescribed for confidence and for business-oriented people.

The author describes its effect as subtle but significant: easing inner constriction during nervous tension and the wish to control everything, including states of being wound up, panicky, hurried, over-mobilised, and unable to relax.

The author associates the remedy with inner calm and dignity simply because one exists: a deeper quietness and a calm sense of relying on oneself.

## RATIONALE

Nux vomica is made from seeds containing strychnine.
Strychnine can cause marked sensitivity to stimuli and muscular spasm and activates the nervous system.
In the author's homeopathic observation, the remedy is associated with the opposite experience: less reactivity to stimuli and less muscular tension.

## ARCHETYPE

The Warrior / Controller.
A person can mobilise, act and overcome obstacles, but has difficulty stopping their own will.

## INDICATIONS — AUTHOR'S OBSERVATIONS

- Irritability
- Impatience
- Inner hurry
- Wish to control everything
- Difficulty stopping
- Work overload
- Sensitivity to noise, light and people
- Tension in the body
- Working through fatigue
- A feeling of “I have to do everything myself”
- A tendency to keep pushing oneself

## SOURCE / DEVELOPMENTAL IMAGE

The author links the theme symbolically with the period of forming will, roughly ages 3–6:
“If I do not push and control, nothing will happen.”
The resulting pattern is solving everything through effort.

## IDEA

Strength does not necessarily require tension.
Sometimes a result appears not when one pushes harder, but when one stops interfering with the process.

## MESSAGE / AFFIRMATIONS

I am allowed to relax.
I can act calmly.
I trust my natural rhythm.
I do not need to control everything.
I can be strong without tension.
My strength remains with me even when I relax.

## IMAGES

- An unclenched fist
- A warrior removing armour
- A horse that is no longer being driven
- An engine settling into calm revolutions
- A drawn bowstring being released

## EFFECT — AUTHOR'S OBSERVATION

The author describes less inner “must”, softer irritability and hurry, and less need to keep pushing oneself.

## IMAGE / MEDITATION

You have been pushing a heavy door for a long time. The harder you push, the more your body tenses.

Then you notice the handle.
You step back.
You pull the door toward you.
It opens easily.
Not because you became stronger, but because you stopped fighting its movement.

## RITUAL

Notice one situation today that you are trying to force.
Pause for a few seconds.
Relax the jaw, shoulders and abdomen.
Make a slow exhale.
Say:
“I do not have to push harder. I can act calmly.”

### message1069 (02.10.2026 20:15 Telegram public view)

Some remedies the author compares for stress:

1. Rhus Rox (source spelling)
Described for physical tightness, rigidity and bodily symptoms.

2. Coffea Cruda
Described for psychophysical over-excitation, when there is a need to stabilise and calm down.

3. Nux Vomica
Described for mental and nervous excitation, helping reduce over-control and increase reliance on oneself.

4. Colchicum
Described by the author as a deeper remedy for an excessive stress response formed early in life.

The author arranges these remedies by perceived depth of work and notes that not every situation calls for working at the level of early psychological trauma; sometimes the issue may simply be physical overstrain.
