### message244

CARBO VEGETABLES

A valuable remedy that I have recently been prescribing more and more often in the initial stages of therapy.

This is charcoal.

It reflects a state of powerlessness, physical and emotional emptiness. When all feelings have already burned away. And there is some emptiness inside.

CARBO VEGETABLES

Covers the dry, decayed soil of the soul with spring rain. And shoots of hope and opportunity begin to sprout inside.

Life and light begin to awaken in the soul.

INDICATIONS / SHADOW

This remedy allows you to “revive the body” even in the most extreme states of high weakness, helplessness, lack of interest in life, apathy and depression. Helps with feelings of depression and suffocation, fading.

EFFECT

The awakening of life, the sprout of hope.

Detoxification, removal of negative emotions and feelings.

ARCHETYPE

- Resurgent fire.

- Burnout healer.

IMAGERY/METAPHOR:

- Smoldering coals that, with the help of fresh air, flare into fire.

- A frozen plant that receives sunlight and

starts to grow.

- A man surrounded by a fog of fatigue that gradually disappears, letting in the light.

ALCHEMY STAGE:

- NIGREDO (black):

Cleansing, letting go of excess.

STAGE IN BODYNAMICS

- Structure of existence (Period of birth,

Right to live).

Responsible for the basic feeling of support and security.

LESSON

- Burnout is a sign of overestimating your capabilities.

- Learn to find strength and energy in peace.

- Life energy can flare up if you give it nutrition, air and support.

Carbo Vegetabilis is a remedy that helps in crisis situations when strength is running out.

Carbo Vegetabilis

helps restore balance, going from extreme exhaustion to restoration of energy and life.
