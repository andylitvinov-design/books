A valuable preparation that I have recently been prescribing more and more often in the early stages of therapy.

This is charcoal.

It reflects a state of weakness, physical and emotional emptiness. When all feelings have already burned out. And there is some emptiness inside.

CARBO VEGETABLES

Covers the dry, decayed soil of the soul with spring rain. And inside, the sprouts of hope and possibilities begin to grow.

Life and light begin to awaken in the soul.

INDICATIONS / SHADOW

This remedy allows you to "revive the organism" even in the most extreme states of high weakness, helplessness, lack of interest in life, apathy, and depression. It helps with feelings of oppression and suffocation, fading away.

## EFFECT

Awakening of life, a sprout of hope.

Detoxification, removal of negative emotions and feelings.

- Burnout is a sign of overestimating your abilities.
