PULSATILLA

A remedy suitable for gentle, sincere people whose soul seeks love, care, and support.

SYMBOL

A delicate, soft, fragile flower swaying in the wind, seeking the warmth of the sun.

## BASIS

Buttercup/Anemone, a soft, delicate flower.

Symbolizes emotional flexibility, pliability.

## EFFECT

From experience, this remedy fulfills the need for care and love.

A person allows themselves to receive care, to be loved, and their heart fills with love.

## ARCHETYPE

- Childlike soul

- Seeking support

## SHADOW OF THE ARCHETYPE

- Fear of being abandoned

- Dependence on others' opinions

## RESOURCE OF THE ARCHETYPE

- Flexibility, softness, the ability to accept.

CONFLICT:

- Desire to be loved / fear of loneliness, lack of self-belief.

## ALCHEMY

- Transform the water-like fluidity into earth. Develop emotional maturity along with softness.

MEANINGS AND LESSONS:

Transformation occurs in two stages:

1. First

open your heart, allow yourself to be loved, receive love;

2. accept your individuality, uniqueness,

- let go of the fear of loneliness,

- transform the need for love from others into love for yourself.

This is a great Path,
big tasks.

PULSATILLA

gently warms your heart from the inside, provides gentle sensitive support from nature.

For faster working through this stage, rituals and extended alchemical correction through mandalas may be useful.
