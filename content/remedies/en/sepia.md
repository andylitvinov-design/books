SEPIA

There is another interesting remedy that opens the streams of feelings. And along with them, the sense of fullness and brightness of life.

This remedy is suitable for people with the archetype of the "Snow Queen",

"cold businesswoman."

When life is so full of stress and betrayals that the only way to save oneself is to hide, to turn off one's feelings. And along with this, to turn off one's life.

To immerse oneself in grayness and darkness, like Snow White.

Sepia gently opens the heart to new love.

## BASIS

Ink-like liquid - a cloud of black fog released by the cuttlefish to hide from external pressure.

SEPIA

- allows one to clear the fog that separates a person from other people, from loved ones, and even from oneself, one's feelings and meanings.

INDICATIONS /SHADOW:

- emotional burnout, detachment, indifference, loss of interest in loved ones, in the family, fatigue, decreased desire, urge

to seclude oneself.

## EFFECT

- Restores openness to feelings and experiences.

Reduces coldness and opens one up to building close relationships. Restores joy and vividness of life.

## ARCHETYPE

- Cold woman,

- Detached mother,

- Hermit.

## IMAGE

- A woman standing by the window and looking into the distance, feeling tired and detached from the world, unable to feel joy or love;

- A person sitting in the dark, who opens a window, letting in the light;

- An empty vase in which flowers appear, symbolizing being filled with life and love.

ALCHEMY: NIGREDO

- Cuttlefish ink symbolizes immersion into inner darkness for purification, letting go of the unnecessary.

BODYNAMICS:

- stage of love and sexuality: 3-6 years.

Reflects the presence of experience of love or rejection, feeling unnecessary, which shuts one off from contact with others.

Working through this stage restores more love within and in life.

LESSON / TRANSFORMATION:

- Acknowledge your value.

You have the right to take care of yourself and to withdraw from contacts if they contain little love and protection.

- You have the right to personal boundaries and to the respect of your needs.

- You have the right to feelings, both sadness and joy.

- You have the right to be loved.

- You have the right to love.

SEPIA — helps restore inner security for those who are exhausted and detached from life, teaches self-acceptance and self-care.

Sepia brings you out of the fog of separation, returning fullness of feelings and brightness of life.
