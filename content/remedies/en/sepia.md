###message247

SEPIA

There is another interesting prepapat that opens up streams of feelings. And with them comes a feeling of fullness and brightness of life.

This remedy is suitable for people with the “Snow Queen” archetype,

"cool businesswoman."

When life is so full of stress and betrayal that the only way to save yourself is to hide, to turn off your feelings. And at the same time turn off your life.

Immerse yourself in grayness and darkness, like Snow White.

Sepia - carefully opens the heart for new love.

BASE:

Inky liquid is a cloud of black mist that the cuttlefish releases to hide from external pressure.

SEPIA

- allows you to clear the fog that closes a person from other people, loved ones and even from himself, his feelings and meanings.

INDICATIONS/SHADOW:

- emotional burnout, detachment, indifference, loss of interest in loved ones, family, fatigue, decreased desire, desire to retire.

EFFECT:

- Returns openness to feelings and experiences.

Reduces

coldness and opens to building close relationships. Returns joy and brightness to life.

ARCHETYPE:

- Cold woman,

- Detached mother

- Hermit.

LOOK:

- A woman standing at the window and looking into the distance, feeling tired and disconnected from the world around her, unable to experience joy or love;

- A man sitting in the dark who opens a window to let in light;

- An empty vase in which flowers appear, symbolizing the filling of life and love.

ALCHEMY: NIGREDO

- Cuttlefish ink symbolizes immersion in inner darkness, for cleansing, letting go of unnecessary things.

BODYNAMICS:

- stage of love and sexuality: 3-6 years.

Reflects the experience of love or rejection, uselessness, which closes off contact with others.

Working through this stage brings back more love within and in life.

LESSON / TRANSFORMATION:

- Recognize your worth.

You have the right to take care of yourself and leave contacts if they lack love and protection.

- You have the right to personal boundaries and respect for your

needs.

- You have the right to feelings, both sadness and joy.

-You have the right to be loved.

- You have the right to love.

SEPIA - helps restore internal security for those who are exhausted and disconnected from life, teaches them to accept themselves and take care of themselves.

Sepia brings the department out of the fog, restoring the fullness of feelings and brightness of life.
