LAC ASINUM (Lac ac.)

Donkey milk.

Recently, I began working with donkey milk.

This wonderful remedy

restores openness to life! It brings out of the feeling of being a victim and helplessness.

## INDICATIONS

The remedy is relevant for those people who are gentle, try to be agreeable, kind, to be needed, to be loved.

But inside, fatigue, resentment, and melancholy build up.

The remedy restores one's own sense of value.

ABOUT THE BASIS

Mare’s milk — symbol of patience, kindness, and sacrifice.

Historically, this is a means of rejuvenation, femininity, and healing of emotional wounds.

ARCHETYPES

- Helpful soul — gives but forgets about themselves

- Sacrificial mother — endures to keep peace

- Wise guardian — whose heart tires from not receiving enough

- Forgotten healer — gives to others, but not to herself

IMAGES

- A woman holding everyone’s hands, but forgetting about herself

- An eye in which kindness shines, but sadness is hidden

-

A child who hugs the whole world but does not know who will hug them.

INDICATIONS

- Self-sacrifice, codependency
- The desire to be needed at any cost
- Fatigue from serving
- Fear of saying "no"
- Sadness from a lack of love in return
- Shame about one's own desires
- Worrying about the fate of others while forgetting about oneself
- Chronic fatigue
- Low tone, hormonal imbalance

EFFECT

- Helps restore boundaries without losing warmth
- Frees from the pattern of self-sacrifice
- Restores inner dignity
- Provides the feeling: "I can be warm without being used"
- Awakens deep femininity, based on self-reliance

DEVELOPMENT STAGE

Age: 2–5 years
Conflict: "If I say 'no', I will not be loved"

Idea of the remedy

True kindness begins where
you know how to say 'yes' to yourself — without betraying others.

Message and lesson

- You are not obliged to give everything to be loved.

You will be loved — even when you choose yourself

Affirmations

I can care for others without losing myself

My kindness is a gift, not a survival strategy.

I have the right to be needed by myself

I choose to give — from fullness, not out of obligation

## TRANSFORMATION

Before:

Exhaustion from giving, hidden self-devaluation, sadness, dissolving into others.

After:

Returning to oneself, gentle boundaries, worthy kindness, feminine wisdom

Ritual: fill a cup with warm water and wash your face with it as a gesture of returning love to yourself.
