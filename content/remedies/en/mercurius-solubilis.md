### message570

MERCURIOUS SOLUBILIS

(mercury).

An amazing remedy that helps you find your fire in the chaos of darkness (NIGREDO) and get out to the light.

The remedy stabilizes and supports you when you are in the dark. And, like Mercury, it guides you through chaos to your inner radiance.

THE remedy IS CURRENT:

for early developmental injuries:

- when parents are aggressive and manipulative (toxic)

- when the child was controlled, his boundaries were violated (the image of a spy behind enemy lines is formed),

- when brought up in an atmosphere of fear and exposure (living in a mask)

- when you are afraid to tell the truth

- when you cannot express real feelings in order to be comfortable, good, because otherwise punishment will follow.

INDICATIONS:

- anxiety about other people's opinions

- when a person does not know who he is,

- no trust in yourself or others, fear of exposure

- "I have no right to speak"

- I can't feel

- with anxiety and duality, when instead of the truth there are hundreds of masks.

BASE:

Mercury: a liquid metal that spreads into droplets, not

allowing him to be grabbed. Like the consciousness of a child during early trauma.

In homeopathy, mercury heals gaps between personalities: lies, masks, shadows. Removes previously accumulated soul poison.

Returns the authenticity of the Self.

EFFECT:

- The ability to be yourself - without masks and self-poisoning

- Liberation from the fear of exposure

- Restoring integrity between word and body

- Moving beyond poison into authenticity and transparency

ARCHETYPE:

- Double agent - living between the mask and the shadow

- A messenger afraid of being exposed

- Mercury, which seeks the truth, but does not believe itself

- An anxious predator: he feels everything, is afraid of everything, attacks so as not to be wounded.

LOOK:

- A mask with eyes full of horror: suddenly they find out who you really are

- A shadow whispering over your shoulder: “You are not what you seem.”

- A man walking on the edge between light and darkness.

He is outwardly correct, but inside there is seething anger, poisonous thoughts, tension. He sees everything, but trusts no one.

Developmental stage: 5–8 years

Self-esteem, shame: “If I say what I think -

I will be destroyed. It’s better to remain silent or pretend.”

The idea of the remedy

Inner poison is the hidden truth that you are afraid to speak. But it is in it that your freedom lies.

Message and Lesson

Authenticity can be scary, but it is the only cure for dichotomy. You don't have to be perfect. You deserve to be real—and to be accepted for it.

Affirmations

I acknowledge my inner parts/voices.

I'm not afraid to be myself.

I stop feeding my soul with the poison of falsehood.

Transformation

Before: Duality, fear of being exposed, internal poisoning

After: Transparency, aligned self, purified authenticity.

Ritual.

Look in the mirror. Write on paper everything that you are afraid to say out loud. Burn/tear the leaf. Drink a glass of water as a symbol of life and cleansing from poison.
