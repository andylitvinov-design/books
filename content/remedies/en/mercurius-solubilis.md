MERCURIOUS SOLUBILIS

(mercury).

A remarkable remedy that helps to find your fire in the chaos of darkness (NIGREDO) and emerge into the light.

The remedy stabilizes and supports when you are in darkness. And, like Mercury, it guides through chaos to your inner radiance.

THE REMEDY IS RELEVANT:

for early developmental traumas:

- when parents are aggressive and manipulative (poisonous)

- when the child was controlled, boundaries were violated (forming the image of a spy behind enemy lines),

- when raised in an atmosphere of fear and exposure (living behind a mask)

- when there is fear of speaking the truth

- when genuine feelings cannot be expressed to be convenient, good, otherwise punishment will follow.

## INDICATIONS

- anxiety about others’ opinions

- when a person does not know who they are,

- no trust in oneself or others, fear of exposure

- "I am not allowed to speak"

- I cannot feel

- with anxiety and duality, when instead of

hundreds of masks of truth.

## BASE

Mercury: a liquid metal that spreads into droplets, preventing it from being grasped. Like a child's consciousness in early trauma.

In homeopathy, mercury treats rifts between personalities: lies, masks, shadow. Removes previously accumulated poison of the soul.

Restores authenticity of the Self.

## EFFECT

- The ability to be oneself — without masks and self-poisoning

- Liberation from the fear of exposure

- Restoration of integrity between word and body

- Exit from poison into authenticity and transparency

## ARCHETYPE

- Double agent — living between mask and shadow

- Messenger, afraid of being exposed

- Mercury, seeking truth but not trusting oneself

- Anxious predator: feels everything, fears everything, attacks to avoid being vulnerable.

## IMAGE

- A mask with eyes full of horror: what if they find out who you really are

- A shadow whispering over the shoulder: "You are not what you seem"

- A person walking on the edge between light and

darkness.

On the outside, he is proper, but inside — seething anger, poisonous thoughts, tension. He sees everything, but trusts no one.

Development stage: 5–8 years

Self-opinion, shame: "If I say what I think — I will be destroyed. Better to stay silent or pretend."

Idea of the remedy

Inner poison is the hidden truth you are afraid to speak. But it is in it that your freedom lies.

Message and lesson

Authenticity can be frightening, but it is the only cure for duality. You are not obliged to be perfect. You deserve to be real — and to be accepted as such.

Affirmations

I acknowledge my inner parts/voices.

I am not afraid to be myself.

I stop feeding my soul with the poison of falsehood.

## TRANSFORMATION

Before: Duality, fear of being exposed, internal poisoning

After: Transparency, aligned "I", purified authenticity.

## RITUAL

Look in the mirror. Write on paper everything you are afraid to say aloud. Burn/

Tear the leaf. Drink a glass of water as a symbol of life and cleansing from poison.
