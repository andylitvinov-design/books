BACH ELM Remedy

ESSENCE

This is the energy of elm.

A strong, mature tree that holds space and withstands heavy loads.

This is the energy of responsibility, scale, and support.

MEANING

This is the state of temporary overload in a strong person.

This is the energy that helps

return to stability when there are too many tasks.

This remedy relieves the pressure of "I can't cope"
and restores inner resilience.

ELM is suitable

When a person:

• takes on a lot of responsibility
• is temporarily overloaded with tasks
• feels that they "can't cope"
• loses confidence in their abilities
• feels pressure and fatigue

Important: usually this is a strong and competent person
who is simply temporarily overloaded.

The remedy restores the feeling: "I can manage."

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

8–12 years — stage of role and responsibility.

This is the period when a person:

• learns to be useful
• takes on tasks
• forms

sense of duty

If the load is too heavy,
a state forms:
"I can't cope"

CORE OF THE TRAUMA

"This is too much for me."
"I can't cope."
"I am letting others down."

## EFFECT

The remedy gently:
• relieves the feeling of overload
• restores confidence
• structures the internal state
• restores support

A feeling appears:
"I can handle this"

MESSAGE OF THE REMEDY
• you are already strong enough
• the load is temporary
• you are coping
• you can rely on yourself

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

A large tree in a field.
At first, a strong wind presses on it,
the branches are tense, it seems it won't withstand.
But gradually the tree straightens.
The roots go deeper.
And the wind no longer breaks it —
it simply passes through the crown.
Strength becomes calm.

## RITUAL

• take 2–5 drops of ELM under your tongue
• place your hand on your chest
• imagine the burden on your shoulders
• with each exhale, the load is distributed
• the body

aligns

• an internal support appears

Say the phrases:

• I can cope

• my strength is sufficient

• I am stable

• I can rely on myself

⸻

Experience shows that the medication gives a feeling of regaining support.

Overload and doubt disappear, and a feeling appears:

"I am back in my place and can hold my life."
