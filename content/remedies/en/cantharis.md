Spanish fly

(Cantharis preparation).

Spanish fly is a legendary aphrodisiac that was used until the 20th century.

The Marquis de Sade used it in his orgies.

Many members of high society added it to sweets.

But the substance in excess affects the mucous membranes and causes burning.

This is how Casanova ruined several of his lovers.

And the Borgia family used this poison to eliminate dissenters.

__

In homeopathy, information about this remedy is used to relieve burning of the mucous membranes and skin.

From the point of view of psychotherapy, it is good for eliminating "internal itching," nervousness, and impatience.

It adds inner calm and dignity.
