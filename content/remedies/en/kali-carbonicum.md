KALI CARBONICUM

This is a remedy that adds social courage.

I will explain with an example:

- Calcarea Carbonica:
I am safe, I am protected, I can express myself.

- Magnesium Carbonicum:
I am valuable, I deserve to achieve this goal.

- Kalium Carbonicum: I will reach this goal, no matter what others think. I see and I go.

The remedy restores the inner framework, dignity, and a sense of "I have the right."

Increases confidence in achieving goals

ABOUT AGAIN

* Potassium — inner backbone, mineral of will, regulates muscle tone.

* Carbonic acid — breathing, the connection between the inner and the outer.

In homeopathy, it provides restoration of inner support, order, and boundaries, especially if a person is exhausted.

## INDICATIONS

- fear of boundary destruction

- fear of losing control

- fear of losing loved ones

- loneliness, lack of support, which is hidden behind caring behavior

- fear of being expelled from the family, losing support

- inability

cry and ask

- restraint, but inside, fatigue, sadness

- perfectionism, but inside, fear of weakness

- anxious composure

- anxious sleep

## EFFECT

Strengthening the inner center and the feeling of "I can"

Restoration of dignity and the right to be oneself without overexertion

Calmness without rigidity

Releases bodily tension, "back armor"

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

Age: 5–8 years

Theme: self-worth, ability to set personal boundaries.

Fear of being weak, vulnerable, unloved.

Conflict: "To be respected, I must be perfect, strong, faultless"

Archetypes

🔸 Stone boulder with a crack — fortress outside, fragility inside

🔸 Older brother/sister — who "holds everyone together" and has no right to weakness

🔸 Unnoticed hero — does a lot but is afraid to ask for help

🔸 Inner controller — keeps everything in check, but is afraid to let go.

Images

🌿 Person standing straight, but with a tired

back

🌿 A tower that begins to tremble from internal tension

🌿 A metal belt that holds the body but makes it hard to breathe

🌿 A fortress in which no one lives, but the walls stand.

Idea of the remedy

Strength is not rigidity.

Strength is resilience, confidence, and reliance on oneself, even in weakness.

Message and lesson

You can be strong—and still be alive.

You are not obligated to hold everything.

You have the right to rest, support, and honesty with yourself.

Affirmations

I am strong, even when I ask for help.

I have the right to be myself, not an ideal.

I can let go of control—and remain dignified.

My support is within me.

## TRANSFORMATION

Before: rigidity, anxious control, fear of weakness, fatigue

After: confident softness, inner resilience, living dignity.

## RITUAL

Stand upright. Feel your spine and lower back.

Place your hands on your waist and say:

“I am not obligated to hold the whole world. I exist. And

this is enough.
