CARBO ANIMALIS

An interesting remedy that fills precisely with the animal force of resilience and manifestation.

This is a remedy of rebirth.

## BASE

Burnt animal bones, animal charcoal.

It symbolizes processes of destruction at the foundation level of the body - the bones. And the way out of deep destructions.

LEVEL OF WORK:

- 3rd, personality structure,

- 4th, karmic and ancestral stories.

DEVELOPMENTAL TRAUMA

- birth trauma: a sense of unworthiness, non-acceptance, insecurity.

## INDICATIONS

- freezing in trauma/grief, physical, emotional exhaustion. Feelings of unworthiness, old age, depression, emptiness. The sensation that life is slipping away.

## EFFECT

- Revives the flow of energy in the body

- Restores a sense of stability,

- gives a feeling of inner warmth, dynamics, activity

- Removes the sense of "fading," fills with strength.

- Gives a feeling of connection with the world, with people, with your roots.

## IMAGE

- The phoenix rises from

ash

- From the ashes, fire flares up

## ARCHETYPE

- Phoenix

- Guardian of ancestors (connection with family resources)

- Pausing soul

- Spark of life.

## IDEA

- Roots are the foundation of your strength, not limitations.

- To be reborn, you need to go through purification.

## TRANSFORMATION

1. Power of rebirth.

✨ Within you, there is an inexhaustible source of energy.

✨ Even when it seems that everything has gone out, life inside you continues to burn.

✨ You are connected to the deep wisdom of your ancestors and family, and this gives you support.

✨ You are capable of transforming pain into strength, and loss into new growth.

2. Acceptance:

🔥 You are not alone. The universe supports you, even when it seems that the world is silent.

🔥 The past is not a burden, but the soil from which your strength grows.

🔥 You have the right to slow down to gather strength and continue on your path.

🔥 Every shadow within you is a place where light can come.

3. Bloom:

🌱 You can ignite your

inner light.

🌱 You can allow yourself to come alive, even if you have been in the dark for a long time.

🌱 You can connect with yourself and the world, emerging from a state of isolation.

🌱 You can trust the process, knowing that dawn always follows the night.

🌱 You can let go of what no longer serves you and make room for the new.

🌱 You can rise from the ashes, gaining strength and a new purpose.

✨ Carbo Animalis is a reminder that even from the deepest losses, new life can be born.

🔥 You have not extinguished – you are transforming into a new source of strength and light.
