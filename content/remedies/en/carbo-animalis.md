### message317

CARBO ANIMALIS

An interesting remedy that fills with the animal power of elasticity and manifestation.

This is a remedy of rebirth.

BASE:

Burnt animal bones, animal charcoal.

Symbolizes the processes of destruction at the level of the foundation of the body - bones. And a way out of deep destruction.

WORK LEVEL:

- 3rd, personality structure,

- 4th, karmic and ancestral stories.

DEVELOPMENTAL TRAUMA

- birth trauma: feeling of uselessness, rejection, insecurity.

INDICATIONS:

- freezing in trauma/grief, physical, emotional exhaustion. Feeling of uselessness, old age, depression, emptiness. The feeling that life is passing away.

EFFECT:

- Revitalizes the flow of energy in the body

- Returns a feeling of stability,

- gives a feeling of internal warmth, dynamics, activity

- Relieves the feeling of “fading”, fills with strength.

- Gives you a feeling of connection with the world, with people, with your roots.

LOOK:

- Phoenix rises from the ashes

- Fire breaks out from the ashes

ARCHETYPE:

- Phoenix

- Ancestral Guardian (connection with resources

kind)

- Freezing Soul

- Spark of life.

IDEA:

- Roots are the basis of your strength, not limitations.

- To be reborn, you need to undergo purification.

TRANSFORMATION:

1. The power of rebirth.

✨ You have an inexhaustible source of energy.

✨ Even when it seems that everything has faded away, the life inside you continues to burn.

✨ You are connected to the deep wisdom of your ancestors and family, and this gives you support.

✨ You have the ability to transform pain into strength and loss into new growth.

2. Acceptance:

🔥 You are not alone. The Universe supports you, even when it seems that the world is silent.

🔥 The past is not a burden, but the soil from which your strength grows.

🔥 You have the right to slow down to gain strength and continue on your way.

🔥 Every shadow inside you is a place where light can come.

3. Bloom:

🌱 You can re-ignite your inner light.

🌱 You can allow yourself to come to life, even if you have been in the dark for a long time.

🌱 You can connect with yourself and the world, get out of the state of isolation.

🌱 You can trust the process, knowing that

The night always comes after the dawn.

🌱 You can let go of what no longer serves you and make room for something new.

🌱You can rise from the ashes with strength and new purpose.

✨ Carbo Animalis is a reminder that even from the deepest losses new life can be born.

🔥 You have not faded away - you are turning into a new source of strength and light.
