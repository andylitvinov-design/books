### message162

NITRICUM ACIDUM

(Nitric acid).

A remedy for mental anguish.

A strong remedy that reflects the state of mental pain, bitterness, irritation.

It reflects a feeling of abandonment, pain and resentment that they were treated unfairly.

These feelings feel like a sting, a burn, or shards of glass.

Emotions that corrode the soul

may manifest as ulcers and inflammation.

If the tension is too high inside, it comes out as aggression and sarcasm.

ARCHETYPE:

- Vulnerable offended.

- The bitterness of the wounds of the past.

The remedy helps

- let go of anger, resentment that eats away at the heart

- clear emotions

- remove inflammation.

APPLICATION EXPERIENCE

In my experience working with clients, the remedy collects the person. It takes you out of worries, out of feelings of loss, and returns you to the state of here and now.

Makes a person more clear, structured, adequate, rational, which is valuable in situations where mental pain and suffering are corrosive.
