NITRICUM ACIDUM

(Nitric acid).

A remedy for mental torment.

A strong remedy reflecting the state of emotional pain, bitterness, irritation.

It reflects the feeling of abandonment, pain, and resentment over being treated unfairly.

These feelings are experienced like stabbing, burning, shards of glass.

Soul-eroding emotions can manifest as ulcers and inflammations.

If the internal tension is too high, it comes out as aggression and sarcasm.

## ARCHETYPE

- Vulnerable, offended.

- Bitterness of past wounds.

The remedy helps to

- release anger, resentment, soul-eroding emotions
- cleanse emotions
- remove inflammations.

APPLICATION EXPERIENCE

In my experience working with clients, the remedy gathers a person together. It brings them out of suffering, out of feelings of loss, returning them to the here and now.

It makes a person more clear, structured, adequate, rational, which is valuable in situations when emotional pain and suffering are eating away at the soul.
