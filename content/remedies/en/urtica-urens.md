Urtica Urens

- a remedy based on the energy of nettle.

Archetype: Fiery Guardian, Sensitive Fighter.

Symbolism: The remedy embodies protection from intrusion — a “prickly” barrier guarding personal boundaries.

Urtica Urens helps with inflammations, both physical and mental.

Mental inflammations: helps when personal space boundaries are violated. Sensitive people, who easily flare up from emotional discomfort, begin to “sting” those around them, like nettle. If they cannot express this outwardly, the pain and irritation are directed inward.

Physical inflammations: effectively eliminates symptoms, soothes burning and pain.

Effect: With the use of the remedy, burning impulses and emotional “sparks” are softened. Inflammations of body and soul subside, and in their place arises a gentle, warming fire of life.
