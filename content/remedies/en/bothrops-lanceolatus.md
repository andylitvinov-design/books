### message749

BOTHROPS LANCEOLATUS

(Botrops, spear-headed snake)

A critical remedy, but it works wonders. Those early parts of the soul that were frozen in shock situations gain life.

This may apply to the stresses of birth and before birth. For example, asphyxia (a child is born with an umbilical cord, etc.).

Those parts of the soul that experience general anxiety from the world and life - thanks to this remedy, they receive support and go into life. And quite active.

🌿Basis

Bothrops is a poisonous snake with a quick, fatal bite that affects blood vessels, causing thrombosis, paralysis, and hemorrhage.

Symbolically, this is aggression, rage, which does not come out, is blocked in the body and tears, leading to self-destruction.

The remedy symbolizes numbness, stopped life, a frozen body, after a strong physical or psychological blow of fate.

🧬 INDICATIONS:

- Conditions after shock, trauma, when the body “freezes”

- Feeling of weakness, heaviness, numbness

ban on healthy aggression

- convulsions, hemorrhages, tremors, strokes like stuck rage.

- External humility is internal poison

- Suppressed attack instinct, fear of one’s own strength

- Internal tension that lasts for a long time and then flares up in rage

- Sharp jealousy, a tendency to an uncontrollable outbreak of aggression.

🌸 EFFECT OF THE remedy

- Helps to unfreeze internal aggression and remove it from the body

- Relieves heaviness and frozen states

- Helps to get out of paralysis, stupor

- Returns movement, freedom, ability to act

- Helps to let go of anger and turn into constructive force.

🔥 Archetypes

A blinded avenger/aggressor accumulates aggression, which breaks out at a moment of weakness.

Paralyzed warrior who did not make the required blow

A frozen tiger, with frozen rage in its muscles

🖼 LOOKS

- A snake with its mouth wide open but unable to bite

- Half of the body is black, the other is alive

- Eyes full of rage, but the body does not listen

🧱 Development stage

2–4 years (Will and Wrath): ban on

aggression, punishment for expressing “I want.”

3–6 years (Love and sexuality): suppressed vitality, fear of spontaneity.

5–8 years (structure of opinions) - prohibition of rage, fear of “being bad,” shame of strength, of one’s desires, of body impulses.

Often - a family history of suppressed aggression.

💡 remedy idea

Your anger is not poison, but a source of strength.

You can act without destroying yourself.

You don't have to freeze, you have the right to move.

⚫Shadow/conflict

I'm afraid of my strength.

If I get angry, I'll destroy everything.

📖Message/lesson

You must not restrain your strength to the point of paralysis.

You have the right to speak the truth, feel your feelings, and be strong.

Anger is not the enemy. Anger is the energy of action when you are aware of it.

Your aggression is the source of life when you own it, not when it owns you.

You can be strong—and clear.

🌺 Affirmations

I recognize my strength.

I control my impulses with clarity.

I choose movement - not destruction.

I see where I'm going. I'm in the flow of life.

I'm moving

free.

My blood flows easily.

My actions are safe.

🔄 Transformation

Before: aggression, chaos, somatic outbursts, fear of loss of control.

After: purposeful movement, clarity, contact with your power, freedom of expression.

🖌 IMAGE / MEDITATION

Imagine that your body is like a shackled statue.

Inside is the poison of frozen anger.

You start moving your fingers, neck, chest.

As you move, the poison drains and the body warms up.

You breathe freely. You are alive.

🔮 RITUAL

Make slow, deliberate movements with your arms and legs.

Light a red candle.

Say:

"My power moves. I am not a snake in a cage. I live, I act."

Feel the tension release.

You are the master of your power.
