BOTHROPS LANCEOLATUS

(Bothrops, lance-headed snake)

A critical remedy, but it works wonders. Those early parts of the soul that were frozen in shock situations come back to life.

This can relate to stresses of birth and pre-birth. For example, asphyxia (child is born with umbilical cord complications, etc.).

Those parts of the soul that experience general anxiety from the world, life — thanks to this remedy gain support and enter life. And quite actively.

🌿 Basis

Bothrops — a venomous snake with a fast, deadly bite that affects blood vessels, causing thrombosis, paralysis, hemorrhages.

Symbolically — it is aggression, rage that does not come out, blocked in the body and tearing the person apart, leading to self-destruction.

The remedy symbolizes numbness, halted life, frozen body, after a strong physical or psychological blow of fate.

🧬 INDICATIONS:

- Conditions after shock, trauma, when

body 'froze'

- Feeling of fatigue, heaviness, numbness

- Suppressed healthy aggression

- Convulsions, hemorrhages, tremors, strokes as trapped rage.

- Outward obedience — inner poison

- Suppressed attack instinct, fear of one's own strength

- Inner tension that lasts for a long time and then bursts into rage

- Sudden jealousy, tendency to uncontrolled outbursts of aggression.

🌸 EFFECT OF THE PREPARATION

- Helps to thaw inner aggression and release it from the body

- Relieves heaviness, frozen states

- Helps to get out of paralysis, stupor

- Restores movement, freedom, ability to act

- Helps to release anger, transform it into constructive power.

🔥 ARCHETYPES

Blinded avenger / aggressor — accumulates aggression that erupts outward in moments of weakness.

Paralyzed warrior, who did not make the necessary strike

Frozen tiger, with rage frozen in the muscles

🖼 IMAGES

- Snake with

with mouth wide open, but unable to bite

- Half of the body is black, the other — alive

- Eyes full of rage, and the body does not obey

🧱 Stage of development

2–4 years (Will and anger): prohibition on aggression, punishment for expressing "I want."

3–6 years (Love and sexuality): suppressed vitality, fear of spontaneity.

5–8 years (opinion structure) — prohibition on rage, fear of "being bad," shame for strength, for one's desires, for body impulses.

Often — a generational history of suppressed aggression.

💡 Idea of the remedy

Your anger is not poison, but a source of power.

You can act without destroying yourself.

You don't have to freeze, you have the right to move.

⚫ Shadow / conflict

I am afraid of my strength.

If I get angry — I will destroy everything.

📖 Message / lesson

You should not hold back your strength to the point of paralysis.

You have the right to speak the truth, experience feelings, be strong.

Anger is not the enemy. Anger is the energy to act when conscious of it.

Your

Aggression is a source of life when you control it, not when it controls you.

You can be strong — and clear.

I acknowledge my strength.

I manage my impulses with clarity.

I choose movement — not destruction.

I see where I am going. I am in the flow of life.

I move freely.

My blood flows easily.

My actions are safe.

🔄 Transformation

Before: aggression, chaos, somatic surges, fear of losing control.

After: purposeful movement, clarity, connection with my strength, freedom of expression.

As you move — the poison flows down, the body warms up.

You breathe freely. You are alive.
