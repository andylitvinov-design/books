### message449

NATRUM SULFURICUM

(Sodium sulfate, Glauber's salt).

A strong remedy that collects a person. It restores the internal axis, structure, clarity, definition.

He brings a person to his feet.

Restores self-confidence and strength to live.

BASIS

Sodium sulfate, a mild laxative, which is used to remove poisons, toxins from the body, and restore water-salt balance.

It is used in homeopathy to remove deep-seated feelings of sadness, melancholy, isolation, and feelings of stagnation.

ARCHETYPE

- The silent guardian of pain

- Witness to loss

- Living Dead

IMAGE

- a man standing in a swamp in the rain. He doesn't even call for help anymore. He accepted the pain as his own.

- A crying child locked in a dark room.

- A glass vessel with frozen water inside.

INDICATIONS:

- depression after difficult events, stress, trauma,

- anxiety, depression after betrayal, disappointment,

- tendency to isolation, withdrawal, self-blame,

- heaviness in the body, head, stomach.

EFFECT

- Release from painful emotions and pain

- Return of feelings

- Restoration of internal support, life in body and soul.

- Strengthening "Yes" to life.

Natrum Sulfuricum is the light that slowly breaks through the clouds of old sadness, giving a chance to let in the sun.

DEVELOPMENT STAGE:

3–6 years – Stage of love and sexuality.

Topic: “They don’t like me for who I am. It's better to be invisible than to be rejected."

RESOURCE

- Deep empathy, wisdom, the power of silent presence.

- The ability to feel the pain of another and be there.

CONFLICT

- I want to love, but I'm afraid to lose again.

- I want light, but it’s more common to hide in the fog.

IDEA Natrum Sulfuricum:

“I’m freeing myself from pain that I don’t have to keep.

I can live again - not as a shadow, but as light."

LESSON

- your pain is not your essence

- you don't have to wear sadness like armor

- You can cry.

You can laugh.

You can live.

- Life and light returns when you let your feelings go free.

AFFIRMATIONS

- I am important even with my sadness.

-

I choose the future.

- I choose to live.

RITUAL:

- fill the bowl with water and salt as a symbol of cleansing; light a candle as a symbol of inner light

- Confession. Bring the thicket to your heart and whisper: “I feel pain, I give it space.” Let the image of heaviness, fatigue, pain pass into the water.

- Cleansing. Dip your fingers into the water and touch your forehead, heart, stomach, removing the remnants of sadness and heaviness.

- Return. Say: I see myself. I accept myself. Life is coming back.

Blow out the candle by giving thanks and letting go of past experiences, opening to new possibilities.

___

The remedy takes you out of the stresses of the past and guides you towards the light of the future.
