NATRUM SULFURICUM

(Sodium sulfate, Glauber's salt).

A strong remedy that gathers a person. It restores the inner axis, structure, clarity, precision.

It puts a person back on their feet.

Restores belief in oneself and the strength to live.

## BASIS

Sodium sulfate, a mild laxative, used to remove toxins, waste, and restore water-salt balance in the body.

Used in homeopathy to release deep feelings of sadness, melancholy, introversion, and a sense of stagnation.

## ARCHETYPE

- Silent keeper of pain

- Witness of loss

- Living dead

## IMAGE

- A person standing in a swamp in the rain. They don’t even call for help anymore. They have accepted the pain as their own.

- A crying child locked in a dark room.

- A glass vessel with frozen water inside.

## INDICATIONS

- Depression after severe events, stress, trauma,

- Anxiety, depression after betrayal,

disappointments,

- tendency to isolation, introversion, self-blame,

- heaviness in the body, head, stomach.

## EFFECT

- Release from burdensome emotions and pain

- Return of feelings

- Restoration of inner support, life in body and soul.

- Strengthening the "Yes" to life.

Natrum Sulfuricum is the light slowly breaking through the clouds of old sorrow, giving a chance to let the sun in.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

3–6 years — Stage of love and sexuality.

Theme: "I am not loved as I am. Better to be invisible than rejected."

## RESOURCE

- Deep empathy, wisdom, strength of silent presence.

- Ability to feel another's pain and be near.

CONFLICT

- I want to love, but I am afraid to lose again.

- I want light, but it is more habitual to hide in the fog.

IDEA of Natrum Sulfuricum:

"I release the pain that I am not obliged to keep.

I can live again — not as a shadow, but as light."

## LESSON

- your pain is not yours

essence

- you are not obligated to wear sorrow as armor

- You can cry.

You can laugh.

You can live.

- Life and light return when you let your feelings free.

AFFIRMATIONS

- I am important even with my sadness.

- I choose the future.

- I choose to live.

## RITUAL

- Fill a bowl with water and salt as a symbol of cleansing; light a candle as a symbol of inner light

- Acknowledgment. Bring the bowl to your heart and whisper: "I feel pain, I give it space." Allow the image of heaviness, fatigue, and pain to transfer into the water.

- Cleansing. Dip your fingers into the water and touch your forehead, heart, and abdomen, removing the remnants of sadness and heaviness.

- Return. Say: I see myself. I accept myself. Life returns.

Extinguish the candle, expressing gratitude and releasing past experiences, opening yourself to new possibilities.

___

The preparation leads you out of past stresses and directs you toward the light of the future.
