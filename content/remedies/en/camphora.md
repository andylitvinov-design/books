###message622

CAMPHORA (camphor).

A unique remedy that works with the earliest injuries of life.

Like a ray of sunshine sliding over the icicles, it awakens the soul from sleep and frozenness.

It returns the warmth of life and light to the hidden corners of the soul, where cold and numbness reign.

The remedy helps to get out of a state of shock, loneliness, detachment and regain the strength to feel and live.

When feelings have frozen, there is ice in the heart, and cold in the body - camphora helps you feel life.

BASIS

Camphor is a substance from the wood of the camphor laurel (Asia). It has a strong aroma that stimulates the blood, breathing, and nervous system.

The substance is volatile and in alchemy is considered “ethereal fire”, dispelling fog, darkness, cold and numbness.

It revitalizes emotions, the body, restores breathing and the desire to live.

Camphora awakens and expands the space for living.

INDICATIONS

- cold and numbness (in body and soul)

- shock, fear, frozen emotions

- melancholy, feeling “nobody needs me”, withdrawal

- alienation,

fear of intimacy

- feeling that life has stopped

- cold extremities, exhaustion, loss of strength

EFFECT

- Unfreezes the body and feelings

- Returns heat and energy flow

- Relieves numbness and fear of intimacy

- Awakens the desire to live and communicate

- The feeling of “I am alive, I am here, I can feel”

DEVELOPMENT STAGE:

Age: prenatal period and 0–1 year (right to live)

Conflict: “They don’t like me, so it’s better to freeze.”

ARCHETYPES:

🔸 Frozen soul

🔸 Man in an ice cocoon (fear of movement and contact).

IMAGES

🌿 Ice cage around the heart, through which heat does not pass

🌿 A huge snowflake inside the chest that begins to melt

🌿 A man in an ice cave who is afraid to go out into the light

🌿 Breath that brings warmth, like the first ray of sun in spring

IDEA OF THE remedy

- Cold is a protection against pain.

But behind the cold there is always life waiting to wake up.

MESSAGE AND LESSON

You have the right to live and feel.

You don't have to freeze to protect yourself.

You can stay warm

and come to life.

AFFIRMATIONS

- I allow myself to warm up.

- I am alive and feeling.

- I accept the warmth of life.

- I come alive and let the energy flow.

TRANSFORMATION

- Before: numbness, coldness, alienation, fear of movement

- After: warmth, energy, movement, desire to live and feel

RITUAL

Imagine that there is a snowflake inside your heart.

Imagine how a soft fire or warm light begins to melt it.

Say: “I allow myself to come alive. I'm warming up. I'm breathing." Take a deep breath and exhale. Feel how life returns to every cell.
