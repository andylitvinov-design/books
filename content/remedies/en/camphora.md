CAMPHORA (camphor).

A unique remedy that works with the earliest traumas of life.

It is like a ray of sunlight sliding over icicles, awakening the soul from sleep and numbness.

It brings back the warmth of life and light to the hidden corners of the soul, where cold and numbness reign.

The remedy helps to emerge from a state of shock, loneliness, detachment, and regain the ability to feel and live.

When feelings are frozen, the heart is icy, and the body is cold - camphora helps to feel life.

## BASIS

Camphor is a substance from the wood of the camphor laurel (Asia). It has a strong aroma that stimulates the blood, breathing, and nervous system.

The substance is volatile and in alchemy is considered an "etheric fire," dispersing fog, darkness, cold, and numbness.

It revives emotions and the body, restores breath and the desire to live.

Camphora awakens and expands the space for life.

## INDICATIONS

- cold and numbness (in the body and soul)

- shock,

fear, frozen emotions

- melancholy, feeling "I am not needed by anyone", detachment

- alienation, fear of intimacy

- feeling that life has frozen

- cold extremities, exhaustion, loss of strength

## EFFECT

- Thaws the body and emotions

- Restores warmth and energy flow

- Relieves numbness and fear of intimacy

- Awakens the desire to live and connect

- Feeling "I am alive, I am here, I can feel"

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

Age: prenatal period and 0–1 year (right to live)

Conflict: "I am not loved — so it is better to freeze."

ARCHETYPES:

🔸 Frozen soul

🔸 Person in an icy cocoon (fear of movement and contact)

IMAGES

🌿 Ice cell around the heart, through which warmth does not pass

🌿 A huge snowflake inside the chest that begins to melt

🌿 A person in an ice cave, afraid to go out into the light

🌿 Breath that brings warmth, like the first ray of sun in spring

IDEA OF THE PREPARATION

- Cold is

protection from pain.

But after the cold, life is always waiting, trying to wake up.

MESSAGE AND LESSON

You have the right to live and to feel.

You are not obliged to freeze to protect yourself.

You can warm up and come alive.

AFFIRMATIONS

- I allow myself to warm up.

- I am alive and feeling.

- I accept the warmth of life.

- I come alive and let energy flow.

## TRANSFORMATION

- Before: numbness, cold, alienation, fear of movement

- After: warmth, energy, movement, desire to live and feel

## RITUAL

Imagine that a snowflake is sitting inside your heart.

Imagine a gentle fire or warm light beginning to melt it.

Say: 'I allow myself to come alive. I am warming up. I am breathing.' Take a deep breath in and out. Feel life returning to every cell.
