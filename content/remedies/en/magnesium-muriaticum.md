MAGNESIUM MURIATICUM

A wonderful remedy.

Its effect is like a gentle touch of a cat. Tender, timid, careful, purring.

It adds a sense of pleasant closeness in the chest, relieves the sadness of loneliness. It restores careful softness and interest in interacting with the world.

The remedy is relevant when there is a dream inside of quiet, reliable acceptance, but the heart has hardened from the weight of resentment, without affection and support.

## BASIS

Magnesium chloride, a compound of two opposites:

- softness (magnesium) and

- the bitterness of memory and resentment (chloride).

ARCHETYPES:

- Independent loner (asserts "I do not need," but longs for acceptance),

- Proud outcast (rejected, but retains dignity),

- Young adult (a support for others, but undernourished themselves).

IMAGES:

- Offended child behind a wall (does not believe that love is possible, but still waits)

- Old room where everything is in its place, but you can feel

emptiness,

- A person on the seashore: the storm has subsided, but it is still cold. In the distance, those he was waiting for are not there, but he still hopes.

## INDICATIONS

- loneliness, withdrawal, chronic sadness that no one sees

- emotional neglect since childhood

- desire to be needed

- at the same time, fear of intimacy, fear of asking for help

- resentment, but inability to express anger

- inability to forgive oneself and others

- constipation (holding in feelings)

- fatigue

Outwardly, the person is calm, gentle, but inside holds onto resentment.

## EFFECT

- softening of guilt, inner rigidity

- ability to ask for help, accept support

- willingness to open up and build new connections.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

- Intimacy (0.1-1.5 years)

- Trauma: Neglect, lack of love (mother is busy, emotionally cold, love is conditional)

- Conflict: need for intimacy VS fear of rejection.

MESSAGE, LESSON:

You are worthy

love.

You have the right to ask.

You have the right to receive care.

Your maturity lies in the ability not only to support but also to receive love.

## IDEA

Strength is not in always being independent.

Strength is in allowing yourself to be vulnerable and to trust.

## TRANSFORMATION

- Before: Holding back emotions, loneliness, exhaustion from being self-reliant.

- After: Openness to receiving, gentleness, restoration of emotional connections.

## RITUAL

- find a row of stones that symbolize feelings of sadness, resentment, loneliness

- acknowledge: take each in your hand and say: "I feel this pain. I carried it for a long time. Now I see it."

- accept support from the world of ancestors, loved ones: hand on your heart, say: "I am no longer alone. I allow myself to be supported. I am worthy of care and love."

- leave the stones behind the house by a tree and say: "I release this past. My heart is open to a new life."

KEY

I have

the right to ask for and receive love, even if rejected.
