### message487

MAGNESIUM MURIATICUM

A wonderful remedy.

Its impact is like the soft touch of a cat. Gentle, timid, neat, purring.

It adds a feeling of pleasant closeness in the chest, takes you out of the sadness of loneliness. Returns neat softness and interest in interacting with the world.

The remedy is relevant when inside there is a dream of quiet, reliable acceptance, but the heart has become hardened from the burden of grievances, without affection and support.

BASIS

Magnesium chloride, a combination of two opposites:

- softness (magnesium) and

- bitterness of memory and resentment (chloride).

ARCHETYPES:

- Self-sufficient loner (claims “I’m not needy” but yearns for acceptance),

- Proud exile (rejected but maintaining dignity),

- Early adult

(support for others, but underfed himself).

LOOKS:

- Offended child behind the wall (does not believe that love is possible, but still waits)

- An old room where everything is in its place, but it feels empty,

- Man on the seashore: the storm has subsided, but it is still cold. Far away there are no those whom he

waited, but he still hopes.

INDICATIONS:

- loneliness, isolation, chronic sadness that no one sees

- emotional abandonment since childhood

- desire to be needed

- at the same time, fear of intimacy, fear of asking for help

- resentment, but inability to express anger

- inability to forgive yourself and others

- constipation (retention of feelings)

- fatigue

On the outside the person is calm and gentle, but inside he holds resentment.

EFFECT:

- mitigation of feelings of guilt, internal rigidity

- ability to ask for help and accept support

- willingness to open up and build new connections.

DEVELOPMENT STAGE

- Proximity (0.1-1.5 years)

- Trauma: Neglect, lack of love (mother is busy, emotionally cold, love is conditional)

- Conflict: the need for intimacy VS fear of rejection.

MESSAGE, LESSON:

You are worthy of love.

You have the right to ask.

You have the right to be cared for.

Your maturity lies in the ability not only to support, but also to accept love.

IDEA

Strength is not always being independent.

There is strength in allowing yourself to be vulnerable and to trust.

TRANSFORMATION

- Before: Containment of emotions, loneliness, fatigue from independence.

- After: Openness to acceptance, gentleness, restoration of emotional connections.

RITUAL:

- find a number of stones that symbolize feelings of sadness, resentment, loneliness

- admit: take each one in your hand and say: “I feel this pain. I’ve been carrying it for a long time. Now I see.”

- accept support from the world of ancestors, loved ones: hand on heart, say: “I’m not alone anymore. I allow myself to be supported.

I am worthy of care and love."

- leave the stones behind the house by the tree and say: “I am letting go of this past. My heart is open to new life."

KEY

I have the right to ask for and receive love even if I have been rejected.
