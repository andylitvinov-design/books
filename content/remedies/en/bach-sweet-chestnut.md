Препарат BACH SWEET CHESTNUT

From experience, this remedy carries a state of a kind of enlightenment, the vibrations of a person are raised.

BOTTOM LINE

This is the energy of sweet chestnut.

A tree that goes through a strong internal tension and opens up after it.

This is the energy of the limiting point – and the breakthrough through it.

MEANING

This is a state of extreme internal crisis.

This is the energy that helps

to go through the "darkest night" and come out into the light.

This remedy works when:

• I no longer have the strength to endure

• Everything inside is compressed to the limit

• Feeling of a dead end

• inner cry

SWEET CHESTNUT fits

When a person:

• experiences strong internal tension

• Feels despair

• feels that "it is impossible to go on"

• is on the verge of an internal breakdown

• is experiencing a deep existential crisis

The remedy gives a breakthrough and transformation.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

Deep crisis stages (often

stage of identity or personality transformation).

This is the moment:

• destruction of the old structure

• transition to a new level

State:

"the old no longer works, the new is not yet here"

CORE OF THE TRAUMA

"I can’t anymore."

"This is the end."

"I am in complete deadlock."

## EFFECT

The substance:

• supports a person at the point of maximum tension

• opens an internal exit

• turns a crisis into transformation

• returns light and meaning

A feeling arises:

"I went through this and became different"

MESSAGE OF THE SUBSTANCE

• even this can be endured

• there is an exit beyond

• darkness is part of the transition

• you are not destroyed — you are transforming

IMAGE OF TRANSFORMATION

Dark night.

Complete solitude, compression, hopelessness.

As if everything is over.

And suddenly a crack appears in the depths.

Through it the light begins to break through.

At first weak…

then stronger and stronger.

And the darkness begins to dissolve.

## RITUAL

• take 2–5 drops

SWEET CHESTNUT under the tongue

• place your hand on your heart

• imagine a strong internal contraction

• don't run away from it — stay inside

• a small point of light appears in the center

• it begins to expand

• the light fills all the space

Say the phrases:

• I am going through this

• there is light within me

• even in the dark, there is a way out

• I am transforming

Experience shows that the remedy helps to go through extreme states.

At the point of 'impossible,' a new space and feeling appear:

'this was a transition, not an end.'
