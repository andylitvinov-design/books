ANTIMONIUM CRUDUM

(Bismuth)

An interesting remedy, a catalyst in Alchemy.

It promotes the purification of metals and spiritual purification.

It is represented as a wolf guarding gold (enlightenment).

I became interested in this material because today I accidentally connected the symbol of this element with my feminine and masculine sides.

And as it turned out, it reacted strongly to the feminine side, but not at all to the masculine side.

Curious.

I started to investigate.

It turned out that this remedy carries a gentle transformation effect. Moreover, it works better with themes where there is fear, shame, embarrassment.

It hardly reacts to strong themes.

The remedy removes unnecessary impurities, programs, blocks, allowing perception to become more even.

And interaction more confident.

__

But strong facets of personality can be well strengthened through crystals: quartz, etc.
