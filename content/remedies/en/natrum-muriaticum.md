###message165

NATRUM MURIATICUM

This medicine helps in cases where the heart is drying up inside from lack of love, fear of contact and rejection.

And while the person is holding a mask, everything is fine.

IMAGE

A man standing alone on the shore, looking out to the sea where his tears are hidden. He bottles up his emotions, avoids comfort, and keeps his feelings inside.

BASIS

Salt. Symbolizes crystallization, preservation of feelings and emotions. But the need for love and care.

EFFECT

From experience, this remedy unfreezes the senses. It becomes easier for a person to communicate more freely with others and open up to relationships.

APPLICATION

Grief, loss, disappointment, loneliness among loved ones, fear of rejection.

ARCHETYPE

- Lonely Wanderer

-

The Discreet Sufferer

- Man with a Broken Heart

SHADOW OF THE ARCHETYPE

- reluctance to show your weaknesses, vulnerabilities, so as not to be rejected.

ARCHETYPE RESOURCE

- Emotional depth, resilience, dignity, ability to cope with loss.

POINTS OF CONFLICT

- Desire for intimacy / fear of rejection.

- Need for support / fear of showing feelings

- Inability to let go / need to move on.

TRANSFORMATION:

- recognize that the depth of emotions is not weakness, but strength, and allow yourself to express them.

- learn to open up to the world and accept support.

- recognize that every loss brings new opportunities and let go of the past, opening up to new connections.

ALCHEMY

- Albedo: purification of emotions,

transformation of grief into wisdom.

This is the Path of emotional healing and integration.

NATRUM MURIATICUM

It returns a sense of oneself, one’s feelings and boundaries, gently melts away inner closeness and opens a person to the world and its new possibilities.
