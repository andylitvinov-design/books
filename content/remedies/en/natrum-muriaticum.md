I will tell you about one interesting remedy -

Natrum Muriaticum

In essence, it is the vibration of common salt. Salt has a drying effect.

This remedy works well when a person is "drying out from within." It is effective for feelings of melancholy, sadness, worthlessness, abandonment.

The remedy helps to find the "salt of one's deep sorrow," to soothe and dissolve it.

When working with this remedy, a person is

gradually "released."

They come out of the state of "a grain of sand inside" – becoming lighter, freer, more sociable, and emotional.

### message165 (19.11.2024 11:32:53 UTC-05:00)

NATRUM MURIATICUM

This remedy helps in cases when the heart dries out inside due to lack of love, fear of contact, and rejection.

And at the same time, the person keeps a mask that everything is fine.

## IMAGE

A person standing alone on the shore, looking at the sea, where their tears are hidden. They

restrains their emotions, avoids consolation, and keeps their feelings inside.

## BASIS

Salt. Symbolizes crystallization, the preservation of feelings and emotions. But the need for love and care.

## EFFECT

From experience, this remedy thaws feelings. It becomes easier for a person to interact with others, to open up for relationships.

## APPLICATION

Grief, loss, disappointment, loneliness among loved ones, fear of rejection.

## ARCHETYPE

- Lonely wanderer

- Restrained sufferer

- Person with a broken heart

## SHADOW OF THE ARCHETYPE

- unwillingness to show weaknesses, vulnerabilities, in order not to be rejected.

## RESOURCE OF THE ARCHETYPE

- Emotional depth, resilience, dignity, ability to endure loss.

## CONFLICT POINTS

- Desire for closeness / fear of rejection.

- Need for support / fear to show feelings

- Inability to let go / need to move on.

## TRANSFORMATION

- to acknowledge that

The depth of emotions is not a weakness, but a strength, and allowing yourself to express them.

- learn to open up to the world and accept support.

- acknowledge that every loss gives new opportunities and let go of the past, opening up to new connections.

## ALCHEMY

- Albedo: purification of emotions,

transformation of grief into wisdom.

This is the Path of emotional healing and integration.

NATRUM MURIATICUM

Restores the sense of self, of one's feelings and boundaries, gently melts inner closure and opens a person to the world and its new possibilities.
