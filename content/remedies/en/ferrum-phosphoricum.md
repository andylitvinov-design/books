FERRUM PHOSPHORICUM (iron)

Recently, a new client came to me complaining of weakness and difficulty with learning.

The assessment showed the cause: fear of control, responsibility. It is important to strengthen the inner core, but standard remedies did not give the desired effect.

Activity was not high.

Then I began using FERRUM, and the client started to become more collected, decisive toward their goal.

FERRUM is an interesting dual remedy.

1. On one hand, it is indicated when there is weakness, fatigue, anemia.

2. On the other hand, it is useful for strong-willed, strong, stubborn, sharp people who suppress their aggression.

They strive for the respect of others for their strength and perseverance, but they are afraid that they will not meet others' expectations and will be rejected.

They are afraid to relax under stress and feel exhausted.

The inner stubbornness of such people sometimes does not allow them to compromise, but inside they can

to feel weakness and fatigue.

## ARCHETYPE

- Unyielding warrior

- Stubborn protector

FERRUM.

Provides focus, firmness, determination toward the goal, like the blade of a sword. Relieves shyness and fears of expressing oneself. Helps to be yourself.
