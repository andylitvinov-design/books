NATRUM CARBONICUM (soda)

This is a new remedy in my practice, but it has proven so effective that I have included it in my group of key remedies.

This remedy quickly softens internal tension and opens a person to gentle, pleasant connections.

1. REMEDY BASIS

Baking soda. Softens excessive acidity, in body and soul. Soothes irritation, pain. Gently restores balance.

## INDICATIONS

- fatigue after tension,
- stress, exhaustion, emotional experiences,
- sadness, loneliness, but inability to express one's feelings,
- fear of rejection, judgment, isolation,
- desire to be useful, but the feeling that you are not seen or appreciated,
- intolerance to pressure, rudeness,
- digestive disorders after stress.

NATRUM CARBONICUM
