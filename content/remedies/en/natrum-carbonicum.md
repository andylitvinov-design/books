### message398

NATRUM CARBONICUM (soda)

This is a new remedy in my practice, but it proved to be so effective that I included it in the group of my key remedies.

This remedy quickly softens internal tension and opens a person to soft, pleasant connections.

1. BASIS OF THE PREPARATION

Baking soda. Softens excess acidity in body and soul. Relieves irritation and pain. Gently restores balance.

2. INDICATIONS

- fatigue after exertion,

- stress, exhaustion, emotions. experiences,

- sadness, loneliness, but inability to express your feelings

- fear of rejection, condemnation, isolation.

- the desire to be useful, but the feeling that you are not seen or appreciated;

- intolerance of pressure, rudeness;

- digestive disorders after stress.

NATRUM CARBONICUM

- Valuable for subtle, sensitive people who are exhausted helping others, smoothing out conflicts, forgetting about themselves.

3. DEVELOPMENTAL TRAUMA

0.1- 1.5 years "PROCOSITY",

A period of sensitivity in relationships, establishing contact and pain

refusal, uselessness, misunderstanding.

4. EFFECT

- Mental balance, balance

- Recuperation

- Feeling your boundaries, not dissolving in others.

5. IMAGE

- A transparent umbrella of light that protects the subtle soul from the storms of the outside world,

- A tender blade of grass, bending in the wind, but still living,

- A quiet stream that continues its path without destroying its banks.

Natrum Carbonicum is a spiritual filter that helps a sensitive person not to dissolve in another.

ARCHETYPE

- Silent healer

- Sensitive observer

- Vulnerable Sage

- Invisible Bridge

IDEA OF ARCHETYPE

- True strength is manifested in the ability to remain oneself, even in silence.

- Harmony comes not through struggle, but through honesty with oneself.

9. ARCHETYPE LESSON

-You have the right to be sensitive

- There is no need to sacrifice yourself for the sake of peace

- Even the gentlest of souls need boundaries

- Your vulnerability is wisdom

10. SHADOW (accept, express)

- Unspoken grievances

- Isolation, feeling of uselessness

- Suppression of emotions

for the sake of others' comfort

- Transferring responsibility for the feelings of others

11. Meaning and spiritual tasks

- Accept your sensitivity as a strength, not a weakness.

- Develop energy hygiene - the ability to feel without immersing.

- Learn to set soft but clear boundaries.

- Understand that being yourself is enough, even without constant service to others.

12. TRANSFORMATION

- From dissolution in others - into a healthy individuality

- From silent suffering to gentle self-affirmation

- From hidden loneliness to connection with yourself and others.

13. AFFIRMATIONS

- I can be soft and strong at the same time.

- I express my feelings with respect for myself.

- My place is important, even if I am quiet.

- I create a world inside and around me.

NATRUM CARBONICUM

Natrum carbonicum is a protector of subtle souls who know how to love quietly and deeply. It helps restore exhaustion, cleanse yourself of what is alien, regain your right to be - and be needed, without losing yourself.
