SILICEA (flint).

This is the remedy that allows you to gather and structure thoughts, words, actions, when it seems that the world is falling apart, strength is leaving, and it is unclear what to do next.

The remedy helps to gain resilience, perseverance, stability, activate the inner core, confidence, and clarity of intentions.

## INDICATIONS

- For gentle people who lack confidence but have an inherent potential for firmness and endurance

- In situations of:

internal weakness, insecurity, exhaustion,

shyness, heightened sensitivity,

- Avoidance of direct actions, conflicts, avoidance of pressure, fear of being rejected

- Difficulties in completing tasks,

- Sweating, brittleness of nails and hair.

3. ACTIVE SUBSTANCE:

Silicic acid — a structure that provides strength to tissues in the body.

In homeopathy, it strengthens the structure of personality and immunity. Strengthens the inner core,

helping to push out the foreign, restore boundaries, clarity, strength.

## DEVELOPMENT STAGE

The period before birth and up to 6 months.

The remedy works with the feeling of inner emptiness and insecurity.

Strengthens the sense: "I exist," "I deserve to have a place," "I can be firm and resilient."

## EFFECT

activates the inner core,

enhances willpower

gives purposefulness and crystal clarity

## IMAGES / METAPHORS

- Sand turned into glass.

- A crystal that has been underground for a long time and now shines in the light.

## ARCHETYPE

Core

Quiet warrior

Keeper of the light

## ARCHETYPE IDEA

Strength is not in loudness, but in transparent steadfastness.

Firmness is not in aggression, but in clarity and silence.

## LESSON

- You can be soft on the outside and strong inside.

- You can shine without proving it.

- Your clarity is a light that guides others

- Your essence is stronger than it seems.

- You are capable of seeing things through to the end

- You

maintain resilience under pressure

11. SHADOW (see and accept):

- The feeling that "I am too weak to cope"

- Tendency to avoid pressure and direct confrontation.

12. Meanings and spiritual tasks

- To gain inner clarity and strength.

- To become a "structure" for others — an example, a carrier of meaning.

- To cleanse: body, mind, relationships — of all that is false.

- To overcome the fear of being seen and to reveal the light within oneself.

## TRANSFORMATION

- From fragility — to firmness

- From distraction — to crystal clarity

- From insecurity — to pure inner strength

- From fear of being — to affirmation: "I exist, and I can."

14. AFFIRMATIONS:

- I know who I am and follow my path

- Within me is clarity and strength

- I can be a support to myself and others

- I shine from within

- My light is not loud, but true.

CONCLUSION

Silicea is a remedy for those who are afraid to express themselves, although inside they have enormous strength, knowledge, and

Light. It helps to 'push out' the excess — from the body, psyche, life — and regain clarity, resilience, and inner form.

The key idea of the archetype:

'Clear as crystal. Hard as flint. Bright as truth.'
