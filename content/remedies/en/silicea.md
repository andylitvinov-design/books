### message390

SILICEA (flint).

This is the remedy that allows you to collect and structure thoughts, words, actions, when it would seem that the world is falling apart, your strength is gone and it is unclear what to do next.

The remedy helps to gain stamina, perseverance, resilience, activate the inner core, confidence and clarity of intentions.

2. INDICATIONS:

- for soft people who lack confidence, but who have the potential for firmness and resilience

- in situations:

internal weakness, uncertainty, exhaustion,

shyness, hypersensitivity,

- avoidance of direct actions, conflicts, avoidance of pressure, fear of rejection

- difficulty getting things done,

- sweating, brittle nails and hair.

3. BASIS OF SUBSTANCE:

Silicic acid is a structure that ensures the strength of tissues in the body.

In homeopathy, it strengthens the personality structure and immunity. Strengthens the inner core, helping to push out foreign things, restore boundaries, clarity, strength.

4. DEVELOPMENT STAGE

The period before birth and up to 6 months.

The remedy works with a feeling of inner emptiness and uncertainty.

Strengthens the feeling: “I am”, “I am worthy to take up space”, “I can be firm and persistent.”

5. EFFECT:

activates the inner rod,

strengthens will

Gives purposefulness and crystal clarity

6. IMAGES/METAPHORS:

- Sand that has become glass.

- A crystal that has been underground for a long time and now shines in the light.

7. ARCHETYPE:

Rod

Silent Warrior

Guardian of the Light

8. IDEA OF ARCHETYPE:

Strength is not in volume, but in transparent persistence.

Firmness does not lie in aggression, but in clarity and silence.

9. LESSON:

-You can be soft on the outside and strong on the inside.

- You can shine without having to prove it.

- Your clarity is the light that guides others

-Your essence is stronger than it seems.

-You are able to get things done

- You remain resilient under pressure

11. SHADOW (see and accept):

- Feeling that “I am too weak to cope”

- Tendency to avoid pressure and direct confrontation.

12. Meanings

and spiritual tasks

- Gain inner clarity and strength.

- Become a “structure” for others - an example, a bearer of meaning.

- Cleanse: body, consciousness, relationships - from everything false.

- Overcome the fear of appearances and reveal the light within yourself.

13. TRANSFORMATION:

- From fragility to hardness

- From distraction to crystal clarity

- From uncertainty to pure inner strength

- From the fear of being - into the affirmation: “I am, and I can.”

14. AFFIRMATIONS:

- I know who I am and follow my path

- I have clarity and strength

- I can be a support for myself and others

- I shine from within

- My light is not loud, but real.

CONCLUSION

Silicea is a remedy for those who are afraid to express themselves, although they have great power, knowledge and light within. It helps to “push out” the excess - from the body, psyche, life - and regain clarity, stamina and inner form.

Key idea of the archetype:

"Clear as crystal. Hard as flint. Bright as truth."
